# IT and AI career paths for a university student in Kazakhstan (3–12 months, 20–30 hrs/week), as of late 2026

Method note for the report writer: all findings below come from web-search result snippets (Nov 2026). Direct page fetches of Kazakh sites (digitalbusiness.kz, taylor.kz, kolesa.group, astanahub.com) failed because those hosts are blocked in this research session's network, so figures could not be checked against the full pages. Many salary figures come from job-board aggregators, school blogs and studio price pages, which sell something; these are flagged. Conversions use 445 KZT/USD. hh.kz figures are sometimes "gross" (до вычета налогов) and sometimes "net" (на руки); in Kazakhstan net is roughly 80–82% of gross for a typical employee (general knowledge, not sourced here).

## 1. Which entry paths are realistic in 2026, and what do they pay (KZ and remote)?

### Takeaway
The realistic "first money" level for a junior or intern in Kazakhstan in 2026 is about 150,000–300,000 KZT/month ($340–675), with the hh.kz median expectation for IT people with under 1 year of experience at ~208,000 KZT ($467). Paths with the shortest time to income and the most entry-level openings are tech support, manual QA, 1C (trainee jobs exist and the senior ceiling is high), and freelance AI/no-code automation for local small businesses. Data analysis and UX/UI pay more on paper but have few true no-experience openings.

### Inferences
- Comparison grid. Months to job-ready are the researcher's estimates for 20–30 hrs/week from zero, based on typical curricula. They are not sourced. Pay is from the citations above.

| Path | Est. months to first income | Learning cost | Junior KZ pay (KZT/mo) | Remote/freelance | Entry demand signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tech support / helpdesk | 1–3 | ~0 (free) | 150k–250k net | Low | Few no-exp listings, but low bar |
| Manual QA | 3–5 | 0–Tech Orda voucher | 150k–300k | Some remote trainee roles | Many QA vacancies, junior queue |

| 1C (trainee → dev) | 3–6 for consultant/trainee, 9–12 for dev | free 1C materials, paid courses | 160k–250k trainee; 300k+ at 1–3 yrs | Remote 1C median ~1.18M, but senior-heavy | Steady SME demand |
| No-code/AI automation, bots for SMEs | 2–4 to first paid project | ~0 (n8n self-host, free tiers) | per project 50k–400k (see section 4) | $14–35/hr on Upwork | Strong local SME interest, very low barriers so heavy competition |
| Data analyst / BI | 6–10 | free/voucher | 350k–500k claimed; few true no-exp roles | Limited for juniors | Few junior slots, high competition |

- The fastest route to some income alongside study is a part-time or project-based path: 1C part-time trainee listings, freelance bots and automation for local SMEs, or support roles. Full-time internships (Kolesa, Halyk) usually conflict with classes. That last point is an inference; schedules were not confirmed.
- With ~208k KZT (~$467) as the realistic junior anchor, a single $25/hr Upwork job at 10 billable hours/week (~$1,000/month) would beat most local junior salaries. But landing the first Upwork contracts is hard and slow, and the sources don't measure success rates.

## 2. Free or state-subsidized training programs in Kazakhstan

### Takeaway
The main free options are Tomorrow School (the successor to Alem School: free, peer-to-peer 01 Edu format, cognitive tests plus a ~26–27-day "piscine", two-year program, expanding to 14 regions) and Astana Hub's Tech Orda vouchers (up to 500,000 KZT for courses of 6+ months, ages 18–45). The 2026 Tech Orda season closed in September 2026, so the next chance is likely the 2027 season. Smaller free options are EPAM's online trainee programs, Skills Enbek, the Google certificates on Coursera, and corporate academies (Kolesa, Halyk, Kaspi).

### Cited Findings
**Tech Orda (Astana Hub, government vouchers)**
- 2026 was the 5th season. Eligibility: Kazakhstan citizens aged 18–45, with no IT degree or experience needed. Voucher: up to 500,000 KZT (~$1,124) for in-person study, up to 400,000 KZT online or hybrid. Up to 5 applications, but funding for one course only. Requires an EDS (ЭЦП) and goes through astanahub.com. Covers accredited schools, with courses of at least 6 months — [astanahub.com/techorda](https://astanahub.com/en/techorda/); [raem.kz](https://raem.kz/tech-orda-2026-kak-poluchit-do-500-tysyach-tenge-na-it-kurs-i-ne-ostatsya-dolzhnym-shkole/)
- 2026 dates conflict: one source says the deadline was 7 Sep 2026; Astana Hub lists 7 Aug–21 Sep 2026. RAEM.kz says admissions are closed — [raem.kz](https://raem.kz/grant/tech-orda-2026/); [digitalbusiness.kz](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-08-21/kuda-podavat-zayavku-na-tech-orda-i-poluchit-do-500-tisyach-tenge-na-it-obuchenie/)
- Some schools add conditions. For example, Product Masters requires that you haven't had a Tech Orda grant before and charges a penalty for not finishing the course — [productmasters.kz](https://productmasters.kz/techorda). RAEM warns about "not ending up owing the school" — [raem.kz](https://raem.kz/tech-orda-2026-kak-poluchit-do-500-tysyach-tenge-na-it-kurs-i-ne-ostatsya-dolzhnym-shkole/)
- Digital Business (Aug 2026) framed it as "learn for free, earn 700k+ KZT" with graduates placed at Kaspi, Halyk and others. That is marketing framing — [digitalbusiness.kz](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-08-18/kazahstantsi-mogut-besplatno-otuchitsya-na-it-professiyu-i-ustroitsya-v-kaspi-halyk-i-drugie-kompanii/)
- Hotline 1818 is given for questions — [search summary of Tech Orda sources](https://www.qonaev-gorod.kz/news/4143347/kazahstancy-smogut-besplatno-osvoit-vostrebovannye-it-professii-po-programme-tech-orda)

**Tomorrow School / Alem School**
- Tomorrow School is free, runs on the 01 Edu platform, and the Astana campus is based at Astana Hub. No programming experience or diploma required — [the-tech.kz](https://the-tech.kz/kak-rabotaet-tomorrow-school-i-kto-mozhet-stat-ee-studentom/); [noks.kz](https://noks.kz/news/v-ust-kamenogorske-mozhno-besplatno-osvoit-programmirovanie-i-iskusstvennyy-intellekt)
- Age: 18+ and readiness for a two-year program in one source; from 15 in Ust-Kamenogorsk — [er10.kz](https://er10.kz/read/it-novosti/v-it-s-nulja-otkryta-registracija-v-tomorrow-school/); [noks.kz](https://noks.kz/news/v-ust-kamenogorske-mozhno-besplatno-osvoit-programmirovanie-i-iskusstvennyy-intellekt)
- Admission: online cognitive games, then a questionnaire, then a Check-In event, then a contract for the "piscine" (~26–27 days). High-scoring out-of-town students in Astana may get subsidized housing — [the-tech.kz](https://the-tech.kz/kak-rabotaet-tomorrow-school-i-kto-mozhet-stat-ee-studentom/)
- President Tokayev launched a Tomorrow School network in 14 regions. A university based on alem.ai was planned to start on 1 Sep 2026 — [gov.kz](https://www.gov.kz/org/maidd/press/news/details/1208122?lang=ru); [profit.kz](https://profit.kz/news/73575/Unikalnaya-IT-shkola-bez-prepodavatelej-Tomorrow-School-masshtabiruetsya-po-vsej-strane/)
- Karaganda 4th intake: registration until 13 Nov, selection 16 Nov–12 Dec (year not stated) — [novoetv.kz](https://novoetv.kz/v-karagande-startoval-nabor-na-besplatnoe-obuchenie-v-shkole-iskusstvennogo-intellekta-tomorrow-school/)
- Alem School background (2022): a free school with no teachers that trains programmers — [5q.media](https://5q.media/2022/02/28/vmesto-vuza-kak-alem-school-besplatno-obuchaet-programmistov/) (older data)

**Corporate and other free programs**
- EPAM: free online trainee programs for Kazakhstan, for example Data Software Engineering Trainee (Big Data), several streams a year. These are training, not paid internships — [the-tech.kz](https://the-tech.kz/karera-v-it-aktualnye-stazhirovki-i-programmy-dlya-studentov-i-shkolnikov/); [the-tech.kz IT academies](https://the-tech.kz/7-it-akademij-ot-vedushhih-kompanij-czentralnoj-azii/)
- Skills Enbek: 568 courses, of which 385 are free. A certificate needs ≥70% on the test and goes automatically onto the Enbek.kz resume. 15,700 people got certificates in 2026 so far (Ministry of Labor); Ratel.kz cites 27,500 (date conflict) — [steppenews.kz](https://steppenews.kz/obshhestvo/besplatnoe-obuchenie-skills-enbek-sertifikat/); [ratel.kz](https://ratel.kz/kaz/s_nachala_2026_goda_275tysjachgrazhdaniz_kotoryh_99_tysjach_bezrabotnyhpoluchili_sertifikaty_o_prohozhdenii_onlajn_kursov)
- Coursera via enbek.kz: free for applicants through the electronic labor exchange (32,000+ applied), but the campaign date is unclear and may be a past year — [elorda.info](https://elorda.info/ru/novosti-stolicy/4262-6a99c611-c5e3-4540-9ee2-de80fb474396-44365)
- Google Cybersecurity Certificate (Google + Coursera) open with no deadline — [youth.kz](https://youth.kz/article/stipendii-granty-i-karera-vozmozhnosti-avgusta-dlya-molodezhi-p3a6aTsT7oh23Y7ZCk7u)
- Zakon.kz (Jan 2026) listing of free retraining options — [zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/obshestvo/6504337-gde-i-kakim-novym-professiyam-mogut-besplatno-obuchitsya-kazakhstantsy.html)

### Inferences
- For a student now (late Nov 2026), Tomorrow School may conflict with university: it's a two-year intensive program with an in-person piscine. Tech Orda's next window is probably Aug–Sep 2027 if the pattern holds. Free self-study (Skills Enbek, Coursera, EPAM, open courses) is available immediately.
- The Tech Orda penalty clauses mean a student should check school contracts before applying.

### Gaps
- No confirmed info on "Digital Bridge" (it's the annual Astana forum, not a training program, as far as could be checked), Yandex programs in KZ, or university-specific IT programs.
- No independent graduate employment rates for Tech Orda or Tomorrow School were found.

## 3. Internships and student jobs at KZ IT companies

### Takeaway
Confirmed paid programs: Kolesa Academy (2 months, 150,000 KZT/month net, Almaty, several intakes a year), Halyk Start (6 months, paid, 3rd-year students and up), and Kaspi programs (Kaspi Lab, where top graduates get paid internships). Jusan Singularity pays for housing for non-Almaty interns (older data). Beeline KZ runs an internship for ages 18–33 (pay not stated). No pay data was found for Freedom, BTS Digital or Chocofamily. Most of these programs appear to be full-time and in-office, which is hard to combine with classes.

### Cited Findings
- Kolesa Academy (Kolesa Group): paid IT internship with possible hiring in Almaty, 2 months, 150,000 KZT/month net (~$337). Streams run several times a year (one catalog lists spring–early summer). The company says most graduates stay and 94% stay in IT (self-reported) — [kolesa.group/education/academy](https://kolesa.group/education/academy); [the-tech.kz](https://the-tech.kz/karera-v-it-aktualnye-stazhirovki-i-programmy-dlya-studentov-i-shkolnikov/)
- Halyk Start (Halyk Bank): paid, 6 months, for 3rd-year+ students and graduates of IT specialties. Selection: questionnaire, business game, technical interview, mentor — [the-tech.kz](https://the-tech.kz/karera-v-it-6-aktualnyh-stazhirovok-i-programm-dlya-studentov-i-nachinayushhih-speczialistov); [halykbank.kz](https://halykbank.kz/index.php/en/about/career/halyk-start)
- Kaspi Lab 2025 (risk/fintech): ran 23 Sep–19 Oct 2025, applications until 31 Aug, test task by 14 Sep; top graduates were invited to a paid internship at Kaspi.kz — [finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/6519-kaspi-kz-ishchet-talanty-dlia-stazhirovki-po-kliuchevym-napravleniiam-banka/). ## 4. AI automation services for local Kazakh businesses (bots, Kaspi/CRM integrations)

### Takeaway
Local studio prices set a high ceiling for student freelancers: simple scenario bots go from 50,000–150,000 KZT, CRM-integrated bots from 150,000–400,000 KZT, and AI bots with a knowledge base from 250,000 to 1.2M KZT. ## 5. Junior hiring market state 2025–2026 and remote work for Kazakh juniors

### Takeaway
Junior roles are a small and shrinking share of the KZ IT market: ~8% of vacancies by one aggregator (490 roles) and 6.5% in AI/ML. There's a "queue" for junior roles, about 13 candidates per IT opening, and a shortage of middle/senior people. Employers want portfolios, GitHub, and skill with AI tools. Remote/foreign work is real for experienced developers (~12–14% work for foreign or international firms), but the data doesn't show it is accessible to juniors.

### Cited Findings
- Taylor.kz (Apr 2026): only ~490 junior vacancies, or 8% of the market, described as a "crisis of entry" — [taylor.kz](https://taylor.kz/blog/it-rynok-kazaxstana-aprel-2026-polnyi-obzor-i-trendy); the AI/ML junior share is 6.5% (62 positions) — [taylor.kz ML/AI](https://taylor.kz/blog/mlai-inzener-v-kazaxstane-2026-cem-zanimaetsia-skolko-platit-i-kak-voiti-v-professiiu-2)
- One review cites 6,276 active IT vacancies, 82% remote, +20% in six months. Its methodology is unclear and it looks unreliable — [hirekz.com](https://hirekz.com/sectors/it-telecom/)
- An Almaty freelancer guide says there's a queue for junior vacancies while middle/senior are scarce — [uwork.kz](https://uwork.kz/ru/guide/it-frilansery-almaty-osen)
- On average 13 candidates compete for one IT position; newcomers are offered from 300,000 KZT and experienced devs 1M+ — [24.kz](https://24.kz/ru/news/social/755886-samye-vostrebovannye-it-vakansii-v-kazakhstane)
- "Classic juniors are becoming fewer"; demand for ML/LLM/MLOps grows despite budget cuts — [dtu.kz](https://www.dtu.kz/blog-post/chto-budet-s-it-v-2026-godu-trendy-zarplaty-vostrebovannye-professii/)
- 85% of KZ companies already need AI talent and 96% expect to keep needing it for 2–3 years (survey) — [digitalbusiness.kz](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-02-05/kazahstanskie-biznesmeni-rasskazali-kogo-kompanii-budut-uderzhivat-lyuboy-tsenoy/)
- Kursiv (Nov 2026) on AI starting to job-hunt instead of Kazakhstanis (AI in recruiting) — [kz.kursiv.media](https://kz.kursiv.media/2026-11-13/rlzh-iskusstvennyi-intellekt-nachnet-iskat-rabotu-vmesto-kazahstanczev/)

### Inferences
- Honest picture: entry-level coding (frontend, generic Python) faces the most AI-driven compression and the most competition. Roles tied to local business processes (1C, CRM implementation, support, business automation with AI) are less exposed in KZ because they need on-site context, Russian/Kazakh-language client work and local systems. This is a reasoned inference, not measured.
- Remote foreign employment is mostly for mid/senior people. For a student in 3–12 months, the realistic "remote" income is freelance (Upwork n8n/automation at $14–25/hr entry) rather than salaried foreign jobs.
