## 1. The new Tax Code (from 1 Jan 2026): special tax regimes, the fate of ESP and patent, MRP and MZP

### Takeaway
From 1 January 2026 the new Tax Code cut the special regimes to three. Two matter for side income:
- **Self-employed regime.** No ИП (sole proprietor) registration is needed. The rate is 4% of income, paid entirely as social payments, with 0% ИПН (personal income tax). The income cap is 300 MRP a month, which is 1,297,500 ₸ in 2026. Only 40 listed activities qualify.
- **Simplified declaration for ИП.** The base rate is 4%. Local маслихаты (councils) can set it anywhere from 2% to 6%; Astana and Almaty chose 3% and Shymkent 2%.

The old patent and mobile-app regimes were closed. MRP is 4,325 ₸ and MZP (minimum wage) is 85,000 ₸ in 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Base figures for 2026**
- MRP for 2026 is 4,325 ₸ (it was 3,932 ₸ in 2025). It was set by Law No. 239-VIII on the 2026–2028 national budget, adopted 10 Dec 2025. MZP stays at 85,000 ₸. Sources say MZP is planned to rise to 110,000 ₸ from 1 Jan 2027 (a plan, not yet law). The subsistence minimum is 50,851 ₸. — [mybuh.kz](https://mybuh.kz/news/mrp-mzp-na-2026-god-v-kazakhstane/); [ussain.company](https://ussain.company/mrp-i-mzp-kazahstan-2026/); [doodocs.kz](https://doodocs.kz/payroll/spravochnik)
- The new Tax Code (Law No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025) took effect on 1 Jan 2026. — [blog.trustme.kz](https://blog.trustme.kz/ip-ili-samozanyatyj-2026-v-rk-nalogi-limity-i-pravila-vybora/); [rsk.almaty.kz](https://rsk.almaty.kz/ru/news/3586)
- The government (primeminister.kz) says the number of special tax regimes has been cut to three. — [primeminister.kz](https://primeminister.kz/en/news/how-to-choose-a-tax-regime-in-2026-entrepreneurs-must-submit-a-notification-by-march-1-30979)

**Self-employed regime (режим для самозанятых)**
- **Income cap:** 300 MRP a month, which is 1,297,500 ₸ in 2026.
- **Rate:** 4% of income, with ИПН at 0%. The 4% is social payments only. Several sources split it as 1% each to ОПВ (pension contribution), ОПВР (employer pension contribution), СО (social insurance contribution) and ВОСМС (health insurance). — [cdb.kz](https://cdb.kz/sistema/novosti/o_novom_rezhime_nalogooblozheniya_dlya_samozanyatykh_v_2026_godu/); [calk.kz](https://calk.kz/calculator/self-employed/); [flextime.pro](https://flextime.pro/blog/spetsialnii-nalogovii-rezhim-dlya-samoza)
  - One source on taxi drivers and couriers gives a different split: 2% pension, 1% medical insurance and 1% social insurance. The total is still 4% and ИПН is still 0%. — [os-taxi.ru](https://os-taxi.ru/news/v-kazahstane-voditeli-taksi-i-kurery-v-2026-godu-perekhodyat-na-novyj-nalogovyj-rezhim/)
- **Allowed activities:** a list of 40, approved by Government Decree No. 994 of 21 Nov 2025.
- **Restrictions:** no employees allowed, and no ИП registration needed.
- **Legal basis:** Chapter 77 of the Code, Arts. 718–720 (some sources say 718–721). — [blog.trustme.kz](https://blog.trustme.kz/ip-ili-samozanyatyj-2026-v-rk-nalogi-limity-i-pravila-vybora/); [mg-partner.com](https://mg-partner.com/novosti/samozanyatye-v-2026-godu-novyj-razreshitelnyj-spisok-izmeneniya-v-sotrudnichestve-i-klyuchevye-riski-dlya-biznesa)
- **Payment deadline:** 4% is due by the 25th of the following month. — [b2b.telecom.kz](https://b2b.telecom.kz/ru/news/business/samozanyatyy-v-kazahstane-v-2026-godu-kto-eto-kakie-usloviya-i-kak-nachat-rabotat); [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/nalogi-samozanyatykh-kaspi-srok/)
- **Number of self-employed:** 193,000 as of 1 Feb 2026. — [b2b.telecom.kz](https://b2b.telecom.kz/ru/news/business/samozanyatyy-v-kazahstane-v-2026-godu-kto-eto-kakie-usloviya-i-kak-nachat-rabotat) (one secondary source)
- **Official confirmation:** the Ministry of Finance Q&A confirms that from 1 Jan 2026 people can work under the self-employed regime, giving hairdressers and beauty services as an example. — [gov.kz Минфин Q&A](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/minfin/documents/details/1030415?lang=ru)

**Simplified declaration (упрощённая декларация, СНР на основе упрощённой декларации)**
- **Rate and range:** the base rate is 4%. Local маслихаты can set lower or higher rates, from 2% to 6%. One source says no маслихат used 6% in 2026.
- **Regional rates:**
  - Astana: 3%, Maslikhat decision No. 349/46-VIII.
  - Almaty: 3%, decision No. 256, both dated 28 Nov 2025.
  - Shymkent, Ust-Kamenogorsk and Ridder: 2%.
  - Lists of other regions differ between publications. — [pro1c.kz](https://pro1c.kz/articles/prochee/aktualnyy-spisok-maslikhatov-ponizivshikh-stavku-po-snr-na-osnove-uproshchennoy-deklaratsii/); [bes.media](https://bes.media/news/v-regionah-kazahstana-snizili-nalog-po-uproshyonke-na-2026-god/); [tsnik.kz](https://tsnik.kz/news/polnyy-spisok-regionov-so-snizhennoy-stavkoy-kpn-i-ipn-po-uproshchenke-na-2026-god/); [centeraktiv.kz](https://centeraktiv.kz/poleznoe/informatsiya-dlya-biznesa/stavka-po-uproshchenke-v-2026-godu.html)
- **Annual turnover cap:** 600,000 MRP, which is about 2.595 bn ₸ at an MRP of 4,325. — [blog.trustme.kz](https://blog.trustme.kz/ip-ili-samozanyatyj-2026-v-rk-nalogi-limity-i-pravila-vybora/); [aksenta.kz](https://aksenta.kz/blog/uproshchenka-2026-kazahstan) (blog sources only, so verify)
- **Proposal, not law:** the government discussed a single 2–3% simplified rate for the whole country. — [nur.kz](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/fines-and-taxes/2449977-ideyu-vvesti-edinuyu-nalogovuyu-stavku-v-2-3-po-uproshchenke-rassmotreli-v-pravitelstve-kazahstana/); [el.kz](https://el.kz/ru/edinuyu-stavku-naloga-po-uproschyonke-predlozhili-vvesti-po-vsemu-kazahstanu_400053816/)

**What happened to the patent, the mobile-app regime and ESP**
- **Choosing a regime:** every ИП had to notify the tax office of its chosen 2026 regime by 1 March 2026. Those who did not were moved automatically to the general regime from 1 Jan 2026. — [primeminister.kz](https://primeminister.kz/en/news/how-to-choose-a-tax-regime-in-2026-entrepreneurs-must-submit-a-notification-by-march-1-30979)
- **Patent and mobile-app ИП:** from 1 March 2026 the tax authorities deregister ИП that used the patent regime or the special mobile-app regime in 2025. The deregistration takes effect from 1 Jan 2026. KGD Almaty (the city tax department) issued the same warning about patent and app users who miss the 1 March notice. — [primeminister.kz](https://primeminister.kz/en/news/how-to-choose-a-tax-regime-in-2026-entrepreneurs-must-submit-a-notification-by-march-1-30979); [almaty.kgd.gov.kz](https://almaty.kgd.gov.kz/en/node/160378)
- **Effect:** the patent and mobile-app regimes were abolished and replaced by the self-employed regime (Ch. 77). — [blog.trustme.kz](https://blog.trustme.kz/ip-ili-samozanyatyj-2026-v-rk-nalogi-limity-i-pravila-vybora/)
- **ESP (единый совокупный платёж, the old single aggregate payment):** Kazpravda ran a headline saying ESP's period of validity has ended. I could not read the article text. — [kazpravda.kz](https://kazpravda.kz/n/period-deystviya-esp-zavershilsya-v-kazahstane/)

**Personal income tax (ИПН) on other income**
- **Progressive scale from 1 Jan 2026:** 10% on annual income up to 8,500 MRP (36,762,500 ₸ in 2026) and 15% on the part above it.
- **Scope:** KGD says the scale applies to all income except dividends and the income of ИП on the general regime.
- **Basic deduction:** 30 MRP a month (12,975 ₸), replacing the old 14 MRP deduction. — [inform.kz (KGD)](https://www.inform.kz/ru/kto-v-kazahstane-osvobozhdaetsya-ot-podohodnogo-naloga-rasskazali-v-kgd-8b3e17); [arqabuh.kz](https://arqabuh.kz/blog/progressivnaya-shkala-ipn-s-2026-goda-stavki-10-i-15-raschyot-i-porog-8-500-mrp); [trustme.kz](https://trustme.kz/progressivnaya-shkala-ipn-v-kazahstane-2026-stavki-10-i-15-raschyot-vychety/)

### Inferences
- ESP was a pre-2026 regime. To my knowledge it was in force from 2019 to the end of 2023. The Kazpravda headline fits that, but I could not confirm the end date. Either way, ESP is not an option in 2026.
- For someone with small side income (a hairdresser, tutor, courier and similar), the self-employed regime is the easiest legal route. There is no ИП, no declaration and a flat 4%, provided the activity is on the list of 40 and income stays under about 1.3 m ₸ a month.

### Gaps
- I did not see the full list of 40 activities in Decree No. 994. In particular I could not confirm whether IT, design or remote freelance work is on it.
- What happens if a self-employed person goes over 300 MRP a month (move to ИП, penalties)? Not found.
- The 600,000 MRP cap and the employee limit for the simplified declaration come only from blogs. Also unverified: a claim that from September 2025 a threshold of 360 MRP a year (about 1,557,000 ₸) applies, above which ИП registration becomes mandatory. — [itsheff/ calk / 003.kz snippets](https://003.kz/ru/articles/kak-otkryt-ip-v-kazahstane-2026)
- I could not open the text of the ESP article.

## 2. Platform employment (couriers, taxi drivers)

### Takeaway
Platform workers (taxi drivers, couriers) can work without ИП as self-employed people. They pay 4% social payments with 0% ИПН, and the platform acts as tax agent, withholding and remitting on their behalf. The tax base is the full order price before the platform's commission. About 1.1 million people work through platforms.

### Cited Findings
- **Rates:** platform workers pay only social payments of 1% each to ОПВ, ОПВР, СО and ОСМС (health insurance), 4% of income in total. ИПН is 0%. — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/v-kazahstane-gotovitsya-vzryv-chisla-samozanyatyh-chego-zhdat-taksistam-i-kureram); [kz.kursiv.media](https://kz.kursiv.media/2025-10-21/svan-pochemu-v-kazahstane-oblegchili-nalogovye-usloviya-dlya-kurerov-i-taksistov/)
- **Platform's role:** the platform operator is the tax agent. It withholds and remits on behalf of the worker. The base is the full order price before the platform's commission. — [blog.trustme.kz](https://blog.trustme.kz/ip-ili-samozanyatyj-2026-v-rk-nalogi-limity-i-pravila-vybora/); [its.1c.kz](https://its.1c.kz/news/477164)
- **ОПВР:** the duty to pay ОПВР was placed on the platform operator from 1 Jan 2026. The ОПВР rate rose from 2.5% (2025) to 3.5% (2026); this applies to ordinary employers. — [kz.kursiv.media](https://kz.kursiv.media/2025-10-21/svan-pochemu-v-kazahstane-oblegchili-nalogovye-usloviya-dlya-kurerov-i-taksistov/)
- **No ИП needed:** couriers and taxi drivers need not register as ИП; they can work as self-employed, with no declarations and no self-calculation. Former special-regime ИП whose work comes only through platforms can switch to self-employed. — [its.1c.kz](https://its.1c.kz/news/477164); [os-taxi.ru](https://os-taxi.ru/news/v-kazahstane-voditeli-taksi-i-kurery-v-2026-godu-perekhodyat-na-novyj-nalogovyj-rezhim/)
- **Scale:** Atameken says about 1.1 million people work through platforms. About 541,000 of them (roughly 50%) pay mandatory social payments. — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/v-kazahstane-gotovitsya-vzryv-chisla-samozanyatyh-chego-zhdat-taksistam-i-kureram); [24.kz](https://www.24.kz/ru/news/social/781554-bolee-1-mln-kazakhstantsev-zarabatyvayut-cherez-tsifrovye-platformy)
- **Competing proposal:** a proposal (Lada.kz) would treat taxi work as employment, with 10% ИПН, 10% ОПВ and 2% medical insurance. Critics said it would "eat a third of taxi drivers' earnings". This was a draft, not the 2026 regime. — [lada.kz](https://www.lada.kz/kazakhstan-news/159383-v-kazakhstane-gotoviat-zakon-kotoryi-mozhet-sest-tret-zarabotka-taksistov.html)

### Inferences
- In 2026 platform employment appears to be folded into the self-employed regime: the same 4% and the same 300 MRP cap, with the platform withholding. Platform employment seems to be a mode within that regime, not a separate tax regime.

### Gaps
- The exact article number (some sources cite Art. 721) and whether platform workers above 300 MRP a month must switch to ИП were not confirmed from the Code text.

## 3. Registering an ИП or as self-employed, and an ИП's own social payments

### Takeaway
ИП registration is a free notification. It is filed through elicense.kz or eGov with an ЭЦП (electronic signature), or in person at a ЦОН (public service centre) without one, and usually completes within a day. Self-employed people register in the e-Salyq Business app (signing in through eGov Mobile) or, according to some sources, through the "Госуслуги" (public services) section of the Kaspi app. An ИП pays a fixed 5,950 ₸ a month in ВОСМС for itself, plus ОПВ 10%, ОПВР 3.5% and СО 5% on its declared income.

### Cited Findings
**Registering an ИП**
- The eGov service "Notification of start of activity as an ИП" is free. It is delivered through the e-licensing portal (elicense.kz) on a notification basis. — [egov.kz](https://egov.kz/cms/ru/services/business_registration/reg_ip)
- Registration is also possible through E-Salyq Business.
- Online filing requires an ЭЦП; without one, file in person at a ЦОН.
- The notice arrives in the "Мои извещения" (My notices) section of the eGov account, usually within one working day. — [finance.kz](https://finance.kz/articles/kak-otkryt-ip-v-kazahstane); [egovreader.kz](https://egovreader.kz/kak-otkryt-ip-cherez-egov-kz-poshagovaya-instruktsiya-2026-goda/); [sdlegalpro.kz](https://sdlegalpro.kz/registratsiya-ip-v-kazahstane/)

**Registering as self-employed**
- **Through e-Salyq Business:** download the app, enter your ИИН (individual ID number), sign in with eGov Mobile and choose the self-employed regime. — [alataucitybank.kz](https://alataucitybank.kz/business/osim/articles/kak-stat-samozanyatym-v-kazakhstane-v-2026-godu); [b2b.telecom.kz](https://b2b.telecom.kz/ru/news/business/samozanyatyy-v-kazahstane-v-2026-godu-kto-eto-kakie-usloviya-i-kak-nachat-rabotat)
- **Through Kaspi:** Kaspi.kz → "Госуслуги" → "Стать самозанятым" (Become self-employed) → choose activity → photo verification → sign with Kaspi ЭЦП. — [basta.kz](https://basta.kz/schet-samozanyatogo-kaspi/)
  - Contradicted by [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/nalogi-samozanyatykh-kaspi-srok/), which says the regime lives in e-Salyq Business, receipts are issued there, and Kaspi is only a way to pay.
- **Through banks:** one source says Halyk Bank, Bank CenterCredit and Freedom Bank also offer self-employed registration and a special account. — [finance.kz](https://finance.kz/articles/samozanyatyy-v-kazahstane-kto-mozhet-rabotat-i-kakie-platezhi-uchityvat) (single source)

**An ИП's social payments for itself in 2026**
- ВОСМС: fixed 5,950 ₸ a month (5% of 1.4 MZP).
- ОПВ: 10% of declared income, with a base from 1 to 50 MZP (85,000–4,250,000 ₸).
- ОПВР: 3.5%.
- СО: 5%, with a base of up to 7 MZP (595,000 ₸).
- Sources disagree on the СО rate and caps for employees. — [itsheff.kz](https://itsheff.kz/howto/stati/platezhi-ip-za-sebya-v-2026-godu-polnyy-razbor-dlya-biznesa.html); [calk.kz](https://calk.kz/calculator/ip-simplified/); [sdlegalpro.kz](https://sdlegalpro.kz/so-opv-oosms-i-vosms-v-kazahstane-v-2026-godu/)

### Inferences
- A rough minimum for an ИП on the simplified declaration, declaring 1 MZP: ОПВ 8,500 + СО 4,250 + ВОСМС 5,950, about 18,700 ₸ a month, before the 3–4% tax on turnover and any ОПВР. This is my own arithmetic, not a sourced figure.
- The 3.5% ОПВР for an ИП paying for itself is unverified.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed from official sources: whether ИП registration can be done directly in the Kaspi or Halyk apps in 2026, and whether ОПВР for an ИП paying for itself is mandatory in 2026.

## 4. Foreign freelance income (Upwork, Fiverr), currency control and crypto

### Takeaway
A tax resident of Kazakhstan who receives foreign income with no tax withheld must declare it on form 270.00 by 15 September of the following year and pay ИПН by 25 September. From 2026 the rate is 10%, or 15% on the part above 8,500 MRP a year. Someone who freelances regularly would logically register as ИП (simplified declaration). No source addressed Upwork specifically.

Currency control: new National Bank rules apply from 19 April 2026. Individuals keep some relaxations, such as transfers up to $10,000 without opening an account.

Crypto: the Law "On Digital Assets" applies from 1 May 2026. Exchange offices need a National Bank licence. Inside the AIFC (Astana International Financial Centre) the AFSA regime applies. In September 2026 the AIFC court banned several foreign exchanges, including Coinbase.

### Cited Findings
**Foreign income**
- Tax residents file a declaration for foreign income on which no tax agent withheld ИПН. For 2025 income, form 270.00 is due by 15 Sept 2026 and the self-assessed ИПН by 25 Sept 2026. — [finance.kz](https://finance.kz/articles/nalogooblozhenie-dohodov-iz-za-rubezha-v-kazahstane-v-2026-godu-chto-nuzhno-znat); [arasha.kz](https://ru.arasha.kz/6870-deklaraciia-2026-komu-iz-kazaxstancev-pridet-straf-esli-ne-otcitatsia-do-15-sentiabria); [nur.kz](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/fines-and-taxes/2411093-chto-kazahstancam-nuzhno-ukazat-v-deklaracii-do-15-sentyabrya/)
- A progressive ИПН scale (10% / 15% above 8,500 MRP) applies from 2026 (see section 1). — [mybuh.kz](https://mybuh.kz/useful/progressivnaya-shkala-ipn-izmeneniya-vstupyat-v-silu-s-2026-goda.html)

**Currency control**
- The National Bank's new rules on currency operations and monitoring (Resolution No. 29 of 31 March 2026) apply from 19 April 2026. They bring stronger bank checks, mandatory registration numbers for some currency contracts, and a requirement to justify the purpose of buying currency. Individuals can still send transfers up to $10,000 without opening a bank account. — [iris.kz](https://iris.kz/events/valyutnyy-kontrol-v-2026-godu-novye-pravila-nb-rk-dlya-ved-valyutnykh-dogovorov-i-finansovykh-zaymov/); [moore-global](https://kazakhstan.moore-global.com/new-rules-on-foreign-exchange-operations-in-kazakhstan/)
- The underlying statute is the Law "On Currency Regulation and Currency Control". — [adilet.zan.kz](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1800000167)
- Repatriation enforcement targets businesses: 141 foreign-trade participants were fined 15.3 bn ₸ under Art. 251 of the Administrative Code in H1 2026. — [lada.kz](https://www.lada.kz/kazakhstan-news/155403-v-kazakhstane-vypisali-shtrafy-na-15-3-mlrd-tenge-za-chto-massovo-nakazyvaiut-biznes); [uchet.kz](https://uchet.kz/news/razyasnenie-o-repatriatsii-valyuty-dal-kgd/)

**Crypto**
- The Law "On Digital Assets in the Republic of Kazakhstan" took effect on 1 May 2026.
  - It separates unbacked digital assets (cryptocurrencies) from digital financial assets (money-backed stablecoins and tokenised real assets).
  - Crypto exchange offices need a licence. Trading platforms and digital-financial-asset operators must register with the National Bank.
  - The National Bank supervises providers outside the AIFC; inside the AIFC the AFSA regime applies. — [nationalbank.kz](https://nationalbank.kz/ru/news/informacionnye-soobshcheniya/19193); [b2b.telecom.kz](https://b2b.telecom.kz/ru/news/business/kazahstan-vvodit-sistemnoe-regulirovanie-tsifrovyh-aktivov-chto-izmenitsya-s-1-maya-2026-goda); [bank-ombudsman.kz](https://bank-ombudsman.kz/kriptorynok-kazahstana-vyhodit-iz-seroj-zony-chto-menyaetsya-dlya-grazhdan-biznesa-i-gosudarstva/)
- On 15 Sept 2026 the AIFC court issued bans against BingX, Bitcoin.com, HTX, BitMart, Coinbase, LBank and WEEX. In April 2026 the AFM froze more than 9.7 m USDT linked to RAKS Exchange. Another figure, "130 illegal exchange offices closed", has no stated period. — [cronos.asia](https://cronos.asia/news/bitkoin-uzhe-ne-predel-dlya-kriptorynka-kazahstana/) (secondary source, verify)
- On 7 July 2026 President Tokayev signed a decree on developing the crypto industry. — [cronos.asia](https://cronos.asia/news/bitkoin-uzhe-ne-predel-dlya-kriptorynka-kazahstana/)
- Market size: about $10.6 bn in annual crypto operations and about 215,000 users (date unclear). — [tadviser.ru](https://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D1%8F:%D0%9A%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BF%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BB%D1%8E%D1%82%D1%8B_%D0%B2_%D0%9A%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%85%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B5)

### Inferences
- An individual who simply receives Upwork or Fiverr payouts: no tax is withheld, so the income goes on form 270.00 at 10%. Regular freelancing looks like business activity, and registering as ИП on the simplified declaration (2–4%) is usually cheaper and lower-risk. Whether freelance services are on the self-employed list is unverified.
- Buying crypto through unlicensed P2P channels or exchanges banned by the AIFC carries legal risk, and also the risk of being drawn into a dropper case (see section 5).

### Gaps
- No official KGD guidance specifically on Upwork, Fiverr or Payoneer income was found.
- How the currency-control rules apply to an individual receiving foreign payment for services was not covered in the results.
- How crypto income of individuals is taxed in 2026 was not found.

## 5. Scams aimed at people looking for quick earnings, and legal consequences

### Takeaway
- **Droppers (money mules):** a criminal offence under Art. 232-1 of the Criminal Code since 16 Sept 2025, with up to 7 years in prison. 711 cases were registered in Jan–May 2026, mostly people aged 18–29.
- **Financial pyramids:** Art. 217 of the Criminal Code. Registered cases fell to 35 in Jan–Sep 2026, a low since 2017.
- **Internet fraud overall:** 28,851 cases in 2025; 13.1 thousand in Jan–Jul 2026 (−10.3%).
- **Typical "quick money" hooks:** Telegram "investment clubs" and trading signals, fake job and task offers, and recruitment abroad into scam call centres (49 Kazakhstanis freed from Myanmar in 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Droppers / money mules (дропперство, Art. 232-1 of the Criminal Code)**
- **Definition:** Art. 232-1, introduced by a law of 16 July 2025 and in force since 16 Sept 2025, covers illegally providing, transferring or obtaining access to a bank account, payment instrument or identification means, and illegally making payments and money transfers. — [yur.kz](https://yur.kz/articles/v-kazahstane-vvedena-ugolovnaya-otvetstvennost-za-dropperstvo/); [kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/140639/v-kazahstane-vvedena-ugolovnaya-otvetstvennost-za-dropperstvo.html); [kt.kz](https://www.kt.kz/rus/state/dropperam_v_kazahstane_grozit_do_7_let_lisheniya_svobody_1377982137.html)
- **Penalties by type of act:**
  - Handing over a card or account: up to 3 years.
  - Making transfers for fraudsters: up to 5 years.
  - Organising a scheme: up to 7 years.
  - Fines of up to about 12 m ₸, confiscation, arrest or community service are also possible.
  - People who report themselves and actively help solve the crime can be released from liability (note to the article).
  - This breakdown comes from a lawyer's site; the exact МРП figures need checking in the Criminal Code text. — [zhumazhanov.kz](https://zhumazhanov.kz/stati/consultation-73.html); [bank-ombudsman.kz](https://bank-ombudsman.kz/czena-legkih-deneg-kak-obychnaya-podrabotka-mozhet-prevratit-cheloveka-v-droppera/)
- **Statistics:**
  - Committee on Legal Statistics: 711 dropper cases registered in Jan–May 2026, of which 192 went to court. For comparison, 53 cases were registered in the incomplete four months of 2025 after the article took effect.
  - 75% of those involved are aged 18–29, and 65% were unemployed.
  - Prosecutor General's Office: through the schemes, fraudsters cashed out about 400 m ₸ in five months.
  - About 214 people were found to have committed the offence in completed cases, reported as "more than 200 convicted". — [kz.kursiv.media](https://kz.kursiv.media/2026-06-04/lngr-dropper-statistics/); [kazlenta.kz](https://kazlenta.kz/115533-bolee-700-sluchaev-dropperstva-zaregistrirovali-v-kazahstane-s-nachala-goda.html); [total.kz](https://total.kz/ru/news/proisshestviya/v_kazahstane_za_dropperstvo_osuzhdeni_bolee_200_chelovek_date_2026_06_29_13_35_27)
  - Earlier figures (7 cases as of Oct 2025; 49 cases, 13 sent to court, after the first four months) differ by period and agency. — [kazpravda.kz](https://kazpravda.kz/n/sem-ugolovnyh-del-zaveli-na-dropperov-v-kazahstane/); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/pochti-50-del-protiv-dropperov-kak-rabotaet-novaya-statya-uk-rk)
- **How people are drawn in:** droppers are typically recruited through ordinary-looking side-job offers ("подработка"). — [bank-ombudsman.kz](https://bank-ombudsman.kz/czena-legkih-deneg-kak-obychnaya-podrabotka-mozhet-prevratit-cheloveka-v-droppera/); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/author_news/legkie-dengi-s-sereznymi-posledstviyami-kak-kazahstancev-vovlekayut-v-dropperstvo)

**Financial pyramids (Art. 217 of the Criminal Code)**
- **Cases:** the financial-market regulator recorded 35 cases in Jan–Sep 2026, against 89 a year earlier, the lowest since 2017. — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/naskolko-sokratilos-chislo-finansovyh-piramid-v-kazahstane)
- **Convictions:**
  - Courts convicted 90 people under Art. 217 in Jan–Sep 2026; almost 90% under Part 3 (raising money on an especially large scale).
  - 646 people have been convicted for creating or running pyramids since 2021 (Prosecutor General's data via the First Credit Bureau). — [qumash.kz](https://qumash.kz/news/schet-idet-na-sotni-skolko-kazahstantsev-osudili-za-finpiramidy/)
- **Sanctions:** reported inconsistently.
  - Part 1: a fine of 1,000–3,000 MRP, or restriction or deprivation of liberty for up to 5 years.
  - Aggravated parts: up to 12 years with confiscation.
  - Art. 217-1 separately punishes advertising pyramids. In November 2026 courts and prosecutors were told not to "pile on" extra criminal articles in pyramid cases. — [airpress.kz](https://airpress.kz/economics/finansovye-piramidy-kazahstan/); [kz.kursiv.media](https://kz.kursiv.media/2026-11-03/rlzh-v-kazahstane-zapretili-nakruchivat-ugolovnye-stati-po-delam-finansovyh-piramid/)
- **Examples (AFM):** Travelsystem; the R.O.I. "consumer cooperative" in Almaty; Amanat Drive, with more than 8,000 depositors and over 21 bn ₸. — [airpress.kz](https://airpress.kz/economics/finansovye-piramidy-kazahstan/); [qumash.kz](https://qumash.kz/news/schet-idet-na-sotni-skolko-kazahstantsev-osudili-za-finpiramidy/)
- **Earlier data (date unclear, probably pre-2026):** 69 Art. 217 cases in Jan–Jul, 1.3 thousand victims and 7.9 bn ₸ in damage. — [finprom.kz](https://finprom.kz/ru/article/chislennost-postradavshih-ot-finansovyh-piramid-vyrosla-srazu-vtroe)

**Internet fraud overall, including Telegram "easy money" schemes**
- **Totals:** 28,851 internet-fraud cases in 2025. In Jan–Jul 2026, 13.1 thousand cases (−10.3% year on year), which is 19.5% of all criminal offences and 57% of all fraud. Another source puts the 2025 share at 23.3%. — [standard.kz](https://standard.kz/ru/post/2026_04_v-kazaxstane-vpervye-za-cetyre-goda-internet-mosennicestva-posli-na-spad-433); [profit.kz](https://profit.kz/news/74572/Kazhdoe-pyatoe-prestuplenie-v-Kazahstane-internet-moshennichestvo/); [24.kz](https://24.kz/ru/news/economyc/787066-pochti-kazhdoe-chetvertoe-prestuplenie-v-kazakhstane-svyazano-s-internet-moshennichestvom-nazvany-tsifry)
- **Methods:** fake bank SMS and calls (2.9 thousand), then phishing and fake service ads. Pyramids and "investment projects" accounted for 770 cases, account or domain hacking 182, and crypto fraud 170.
- **Telegram:** fraudsters run closed "investment clubs" that promise profitable trading signals and expert traders, and persuade victims to send crypto to a "manager's wallet". In East Kazakhstan region, about 300 of 900+ fraud cases involved Telegram. — [inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/pyat-lovushek-moshennikov-vwhatsapp-itelegram-okotorih-stoit-znat-kazahstantsam-d4fbce4e); [orda.kz](https://orda.kz/v-kazahstane-chislo-internet-moshennichestv-vyroslo-v-55-raz-za-sem-let-418012/)
- **Growth:** internet fraud grew 55-fold over seven years. — [nur.kz](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/personal/2415987-kolichestvo-faktov-internet-moshennichestva-vyroslo-v-55-raz-v-kazahstane/)
- **Recovery:** in November 2026 the Interior Ministry reported more than 117 bn ₸ of suspicious funds blocked and 5.9 bn ₸ returned to victims. — [centralmedia24.kz](https://centralmedia24.kz/kibermoshennichestvo-kazakhstan-2026-statistika-antifrod-vozvrat-deneg/)

**Betting and online casinos**
- The 2024 law on лудомания (gambling addiction) banned advertising of bookmakers, lotteries and online casinos in outdoor ads, media, the internet, social networks and SMS. It added criminal liability and fines, and barred civil servants and military personnel from gambling. — [Sputnik 2024](https://ru.sputnik.kz/20240422/zapret-reklamy-ugolovnaya-otvetstvennost-i-shtrafy-mazhilis-prinyal-zakon-o-ludomanii-43840726.html); [kaztag.kz](https://kaztag.kz/ru/news/v-kazakhstane-prinyali-zakon-o-zaprete-na-reklamu-bukmekerov-i-onlayn-kazino); [uchet.kz](https://uchet.kz/news/reklamu-loterey-bukmekerov-i-totalizatorov-zapretili-v-kazakhstane/)
- Offshore online casinos are illegal. Organisers face up to 7 years per one source and up to 2 years per another, so this needs verification. — [lsm.kz](https://lsm.kz/parlament-odobril-zapret-na-reklamu-bukmekerskih-kontor); [sovetreklama.org](https://sovetreklama.org/2025/08/za-reklamu-onlajn-kazino-mozhno-budet-lishitsja-ne-tolko-deneg-no-i-svobody/)

### Inferences
- The main legal danger for someone looking for quick money is becoming a dropper. "Earn by receiving and forwarding transfers" or "rent out your Kaspi card" offers are exactly what Art. 232-1 punishes. The victim of recruitment becomes the accused, and most of those charged are young unemployed people.
- Task scams (likes or reviews followed by a "deposit to unlock earnings") and pay-to-start courses usually fall under general fraud (Art. 190 of the Criminal Code), with the job-seeker as the victim. Recovery rates are low: about 5.9 bn ₸ returned against 117 bn ₸ blocked, although the two figures are not directly comparable.

### Gaps
- No official statistics were found specifically for "likes/reviews" task scams, marketplace "work" scams or pay-to-start courses.
- Art. 190 sanctions were not retrieved.
- No new 2026 betting-law changes were found; the betting data above is from 2024–2025.
- No AFM-published list of pyramids was retrieved; the afm.gov.kz site was not reachable.
