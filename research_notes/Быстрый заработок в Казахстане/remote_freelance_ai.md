# Remote / Online Earning for a Beginner in Kazakhstan (IT, programming, AI), late 2026

Research notes. Date of research: 2026-11-26. Currency conversions use about **1 USD ≈ 485–490 KZT**, the rate in an informburo.kz page header seen in search results ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/stati/kak-stat-frilanserom)). For RUB-priced gigs on Russian platforms, the notes use roughly **1 RUB ≈ 6 KZT**. No source in this research backs that RUB rate, so check it before using it.

**Method note:** The network sandbox blocked many direct page fetches: outlier.ai, elevatepay.co, tengrinews.kz and sostav.ru failed to resolve. Many findings below therefore come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not full-page reads. The writer should treat those as "reported by" rather than verified verbatim.

## 1. Freelance platforms usable from Kazakhstan: fees, beginner competition, first-order prices, time to first order

### Takeaway
For a beginner with weak skills and weak English, the fastest realistic first money comes from Russian-language platforms. Kwork is easiest: the seller lists a fixed-price service, there is about a 20% fee, and some beginners get a first order within about a week. Upwork pays more and is usable from KZ, but connects cost money and English-speaking competition is fierce, so a first contract within 30 days is possible but not typical. Expect the first orders to pay only a few dollars to a few tens of dollars.

### Cited Findings
- **Upwork fees (2025–2026):** new Marketplace contracts since May 2025 carry a variable freelancer service fee of 0–15%, set per contract and visible only at proposal or offer stage. Contracts signed before May 2025 keep the old 10% — [Upwork "Is Upwork free" / search summary](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free); [Hackernoon "Upwork fees are basically vibes now"](https://hackernoon.com/lite/upwork-fees-are-basically-vibes-now)
- **Upwork Connects:** the Basic plan is free with 10 Connects per month. Extra Connects cost $0.15 each (~73 KZT). Freelancer Plus is listed at about $19.99/month (~9,700 KZT) with 100 Connects. Sources disagree on the Plus price — [Upwork Freelancer Plus guide](https://www.upwork.com/resources/freelancer-plus-guide); [jobbers.io](https://www.jobbers.io/what-are-upwork-connects-and-how-much-do-they-cost/)
- **Fiverr:** charges a 20% service fee on all freelancer earnings, as cited by Upwork's own comparison content — [Upwork resources](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free)
- **Upwork vs Russian-language platforms:** Upwork needs English. Reviewers advise people with weak English to start on Kwork or FL.ru — [Skyeng: best freelance exchange for beginners](https://skyeng.ru/it-industry/career/luchshaya-frilans-birzha-dlya-nachinayushchih-s-chego-nachat/)
- **Kwork model:** sellers create fixed-price service cards ("kworks"). Commission is about 20%, entry difficulty is low and the interface is in Russian — [vc.ru TOP-7 freelance exchanges 2026 (21.01.2026)](https://vc.ru/money/2116275-top-7-birzh-frilansa-2025); [sostav.ru TOP-18 for beginners 2026](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/291733/95204)
- **Kwork anecdote:** one freelancer spent 3 months finding clients via social media with no result, then got a first copywriting order on Kwork within about 1 week. This is a single anecdote, not statistics — [tenchat "Мой первый заказ на KWORK"](https://tenchat.ru/glebanya)
- **Workzilla:** reported terms are a 1,290 RUB subscription (~7,700 KZT), a 10% commission per task and a 5% fee on withdrawal to card. These prices date from Oct 2024. Another review cites 490 RUB per 3 months, so sources conflict — [QUASA: Kwork or Workzilla for a beginner](https://quasa.io/ru/stories/kwork-ili-workzilla-dlya-novichka-gde-bystree-okupitsya-vhod); [toolfox Workzilla](https://toolfox.ru/services/s/workzilla)
- **YouDo:** posting a task is free; workers pay for responses — [DTF top-7 freelance exchanges in Russia 2026](https://dtf.ru/rating_top/4998965-top-birzh-frilansa-v-rossii)
- **Freelancehunt (Ukrainian platform):** it has projects targeting Kazakhstan, e.g. "Operator needed for calling clients in Kazakhstan" — [Freelancehunt project](https://freelancehunt.com/en/project/trebuetsya-operator-dlya-obzvona-klientov/1483101.html). No current fee data was found.
- **Kazakhstan-local platforms:** an informburo.kz explainer names Megamaster and Allfreelance. It says local freelancing has lower competition but pays less — [informburo.kz "Как стать фрилансером"](https://informburo.kz/stati/kak-stat-frilanserom)
- **Upwork earning ceiling:** a KZ source cites Upwork's 18M registered freelancers and programmers earning $60–70/hr. That is experienced-dev territory, not beginner — [informburo.kz](https://informburo.kz/stati/kak-stat-frilanserom)
- **Astana Hub support:** Astana Hub ran free Upwork training covering taxes and commissions; the material dates from 2022. It also has a "Freelance school" page — [Astana Hub Freelanceschool](https://astanahub.com/l/Freelanceschool); [digitalbusiness.kz tag Upwork](https://digitalbusiness.kz/tag/upwork)
- **New 2026 tax regime for self-employed:** Kazakhstan introduced a new special tax regime for the self-employed / platform workers from 2026 — [digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-03-20](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-03-20/kak-platformennaya-zanyatost-menyaet-rinok-uslug-kazahstana-vzglyad-iznutri/)
- **Old access issue:** an old Habr Q&A thread has users saying Upwork "closed" for them. This is undated or old, so verify — [qna.habr.com](https://qna.habr.com/q/930845)

### Inferences
- **Fastest-to-cash ranking for a weak-skill beginner:**
  1. Kwork or Workzilla micro-tasks (Russian-language, small, fast tasks).
  2. Telegram freelance chats and local KZ clients, i.e. direct work with no platform fee but higher scam risk.
  3. FL.ru.
  4. Upwork or Fiverr, which are slower to start but have higher prices later.
- **Russian platforms pay in RUB.** Withdrawal to KZ cards is affected by sanctions on Russian banks. Kaspi cannot transfer to Russian bank cards, per Kaspi/Visa/MC restrictions reported in [digitalbusiness.kz Aug 2026](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-08-24/chem-zamenit-zolotuyu-koronu-kak-kazahstantsi-perevodyat-dengi-v-rossiyu/). Payouts from Kwork or FL.ru to KZ cards should be tested with a small amount first.
- **Upwork in KZ:** to my knowledge, Upwork suspended operations in Russia and Belarus in 2022 but not in Kazakhstan. I found no 2026 source that confirms this.
- **Budget for 30 days on Upwork:** ~$5–20 (2,400–9,800 KZT) for extra Connects. Many beginners send 30–80 proposals before the first contract; this is community folklore with no reliable statistic found.

### Gaps
- Current official commission pages for Kwork, FL.ru (PRO subscription price), Freelancehunt and YouDo could not be fetched.
- No reliable statistics on median time-to-first-order for beginners on any platform.
- No 2026-verified info on whether Kwork or FL.ru pay out directly to Kazakh cards. Historically, withdrawals to foreign cards and e-wallets existed, but this is unverified.
- No sourced data on Telegram freelance chats (fees are zero, but quality and scam rates are unknown).

## 2. Beginner-friendly AI training / data annotation / RLHF gigs: availability for KZ, pay, verification

### Takeaway
AI-training work does exist for Kazakhstan residents, especially for **Kazakh- and Russian-language** projects (Welo Data, Mindrift/Toloka, some Outlier projects). The real beginner advantage is **native Kazakh language**: one Kazakh Data Trainer listing pays $26.40/hr. Generalist rates range from $5/hr at the low end to $15–30/hr. Access is project-based and intermittent, with onboarding tests and ID verification, and is not guaranteed income. Coding projects such as Mindrift's $30/hr Python role need real skills.

Payouts on these platforms usually go via PayPal (not usable in KZ, see Q5), Payoneer or other providers. Check the payout method before investing time.
- **Realistic beginner monthly income:** from $0 (no projects available) to ~$100–500 (49,000–245,000 KZT) at 5–20 hrs/week while projects are active. Hype figures of "$1,000+/month" assume steady project flow and higher-tier skills.

- Outlier's KZ eligibility is unconfirmed because the page was blocked.
- No verified payout method per platform for KZ.

## 3. AI-assisted services a beginner can sell: demand and prices

### Takeaway
Selling simple AI-enhanced services works on Russian-language platforms: Telegram bots with a ChatGPT API, AI consultant bots, AI copywriting and translation, and AI images. Entry-level bot listings start around 3,000 RUB (~18,000 KZT, ~$37). Knowledge-base AI bots sell for 20,000–50,000 RUB and up, but buyers expect working integrations. A beginner can build simple bots with no-code tools or Python plus AI assistance within weeks.

### Cited Findings
- **Kwork AI chatbot prices (DTF 2026 ranking, flagged as advertising):**
  - Simple ChatGPT-API bot: from **10,000 RUB** (~60,000 KZT, ~$120)
  - Bot with custom knowledge base: from **50,000 RUB**
  - CRM-integrated bot: from **150,000 RUB**
  - Plus API token costs on top — [DTF "Топ 5 площадок где заказать AI чат-бота" 2026](https://dtf.ru/rating_top/4999278)
- **FL.ru listing snapshots:**
  - Python Telegram bot with leads, mailings and admin panel: **2,990 RUB**
  - "Create a Telegram bot for business": **3,250 RUB**
  - AI consultant for a website: **19,500 RUB**
  - AI assistant bot: **39,000 RUB**
  - Sources: [FL.ru боты с AI](https://www.fl.ru/uslugi-freelancera/boty-s-ai/); [FL.ru боты для мессенджеров](https://www.fl.ru/uslugi-freelancera/boty-dlya-messengerov/); [FL.ru AI-бот для Telegram 24/7](https://www.fl.ru/uslugi-freelancera/20691/ai-bot-dlya-telegram-otvechaet-klientam-24-na-7-po-vashey-baze-znaniy.html)
- **FL.ru has dedicated categories** for "Боты с AI", "Telegram Mini Apps" and "Боты для бизнеса", which shows category-level demand — [FL.ru Telegram mini apps](https://www.fl.ru/uslugi-freelancera/telegram-mini-apps/); [FL.ru боты для бизнеса](https://www.fl.ru/uslugi-freelancera/boty-dlya-biznesa/)

### Inferences
- **Realistic beginner path:** offer a "basic Telegram bot / FAQ bot on ChatGPT" for 3,000–10,000 RUB (~$35–120, 18,000–60,000 KZT) on Kwork, or 15,000–50,000 KZT directly to local KZ small businesses (Instagram shops, salons, courses) via Telegram or Instagram outreach. Local KZ SMBs need Kazakh- and Russian-language bots, which is a niche that foreign competitors can't serve easily.
- **AI copywriting, translation (RU↔KZ↔EN) and AI image services** are commoditised. Expect very low prices ($3–15 per piece). The value is in Kazakh-language output and local market knowledge.
- **Prompt-engineering-only gigs:** I found no reliable demand data; they are likely mostly hype at beginner level.

### Gaps
- No current Kwork prices fetched directly for copywriting, translation, AI images or no-code sites (Tilda/Taplink).
- No data on local KZ SMB demand or prices for chatbots (e.g. on OLX.kz or Telegram chats).
- No data on Fiverr AI-service pricing or competition.

## 5. Payouts to Kazakhstan: Payoneer, Wise, Upwork bank transfer, KZ bank cards, crypto, PayPal

### Takeaway
- **Payoneer** is the long-standing workhorse for KZ freelancers (Upwork, Fiverr and many AI platforms).
- **Upwork's own options** are Direct to Local Bank ($0.99) and wire (~$30–50). KZ eligibility for the $0.99 route is unconfirmed.
- **Wise does not open accounts for KZ residents.** It can only deliver money *to* a KZ bank account.
- **PayPal** is reported as usable for some withdrawals to KZ Visa cards, but historically it has been limited and unreliable.
- **USDT/crypto** became formally regulated from 1 May 2026. Legal routes are licensed platforms (AIFC/AFSA and new licensees). Informal Telegram P2P exchangers are risky.

### Cited Findings
- **Upwork Direct to Local Bank:** costs **$0.99 per withdrawal** (~485 KZT), plus possible incoming bank fees, and takes up to about 7 business days — [Upwork Help 211060578 (via search)](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060578); [Wise blog on Upwork USD withdrawal](https://wise.com/us/blog/upwork-withdraw-usd-outside-us)
- **Upwork wire transfer:** sources conflict, with a reported $30 flat fee ([AirTM 2026](https://www.airtm.com/en/blog/digital-entrepreneurship/how-to-withdraw-money-from-upwork/)) versus $50 ([ElevatePay 2026](https://www.elevatepay.co/blog/upwork-withdrawal-fees)). An old Habr comment cites $30 plus about $16 correspondent-bank fees, making wire worthwhile only above roughly $1,000 — [Habr Q&A](https://qna.habr.com/q/245865)
- **Upwork Direct to US Bank (new fee):** from **1 Sept 2026**, a $2.99 fee applies per Direct to U.S. Bank withdrawal for non-US tax addresses — [selfemployed.com](https://www.selfemployed.com/news/upwork-direct-us-bank-withdrawal-fee-2026/)
- **Payoneer for Upwork:** Payoneer has a dedicated Russian-language "Get paid by Upwork" page — [Payoneer RU](https://www.payoneer.com/ru/get-paid-by-upwork/)
- **Payoneer in KZ freelancer discussions:** older Habr threads (~2017–2019) call Payoneer the main option because "PayPal doesn't work with Kazakhstan" and Skrill is expensive and slow — [qna.habr.com/q/245865](https://qna.habr.com/q/245865)
- **Payoneer's own KZ article:** Payoneer publishes a Kazakhstan payment-landscape article; its contents were not verified — [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com/resources/accounts-payable/kazakhstan-payment-landscape-the-best-ways-to-send-and-receive-money-internationally)
- **Wise account opening:** KZ-based residents reportedly cannot open a Wise account, the Wise card is not available to KZ residents, and KZT cannot be held — [exiap.com Wise in Kazakhstan 2026](https://www.exiap.com/guides/using-the-wise-card-in-kazakhstan); [onesafe.io](https://www.onesafe.io/blog/does-wise-work-in-kazakhstan)
- **Wise sending to KZ:** Wise does support sending *to* KZ bank accounts, and the recipient needs no Wise account — [Wise send money to Kazakhstan](https://wise.com/us/send-money/send-money-to-kazakhstan)
- **PayPal (KZ review sites):** reviews (one updated June 2026) say PayPal allows withdrawal to Kazakh bank cards, including Visa. These are not official terms — [finance.kz](https://finance.kz/articles/kak-vyvesti-dengi-s-paypal-na-kartu-visa-v-kazahstane); [moneypanda.kz](https://moneypanda.kz/kz/news/paypal-v-kazakhstane-registratsiya-cherez-telefon-mezhdunarodnye-perevody-i-vyvod-sredstv-na-bankovskuyu-kartu-56); [bank.kz](https://bank.kz/wikibank/finansy/paypal-kazahstan/)
- **PayPal (older freelancer reports):** older reports say PayPal "doesn't work" for KZ, so the sources conflict — [Habr](https://qna.habr.com/q/245865)
- **Kaspi Gold transfers to foreign cards:** cost **0.95%, minimum 200 KZT**; the help page was updated 17.07.2026. Some countries are blocked under Visa/MC rules — [Kaspi Guide](https://guide.kaspi.kz/client/ru/app/transfers/general/q1957)
- **Kaspi transfers to Russia:** Kaspi cannot send to Russian bank cards, but other countries remain available (Aug 2026). Kaspi added another major payment system in July 2026 — [digitalbusiness.kz 2026-08-24](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-08-24/chem-zamenit-zolotuyu-koronu-kak-kazahstantsi-perevodyat-dengi-v-rossiyu/); [digitalbusiness.kz 2026-07-14](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-07-14/kaspi-kz-otkril-kazahstantsam-perevodi-eshche-v-odnu-krupneyshuyu-platezhnuyu-sistemu/)
- **Crypto regulation:** comprehensive digital-asset regulation took effect **1 May 2026** (amendments to the law "On digital assets" plus National Bank by-laws) — [National Bank via search](https://nationalbank.kz/en/news/informacionnye-soobshcheniya/19193); [kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/finance/147602/kriptooborot-vyhodit-iz-seroj-zony-v-pravovoe-pole-nacbank.html)
- **USDT/USDC classification:** a National Bank deputy chairman says they are treated as *unsecured* digital assets with issuers outside KZ. Tengrinews describes USDT as a backed stablecoin, so the two conflict — [BeInCrypto](https://ru.beincrypto.com/kazahstan-brokery-16-kriptoaktivov); [Tengrinews](https://tengrinews.kz/article/kazahstane-legalizovali-kriptovalyutu-mojno-chto-vs-esch-4019/)
- **Legal crypto channels:** these were historically AIFC platforms supervised by AFSA. November 2025 amendments reportedly ended the AIFC monopoly — [finance.kz](https://finance.kz/articles/kriptovalyuta-mfca-licenzii-binance-kazakhstan-2026); [datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/ru/crypto/best-crypto-exchanges-kazakhstan) (commercial or affiliate source)
- **Informal exchangers:** Zakon.kz warns that "buy USDT for cash" or "fast exchange via Telegram" ads do not indicate a legal exchanger — [zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/finansy/6531648-mozhet-li-prostoy-kazakhstanets-kupit-kriptovalyutu-i-platit-ey-kak-obychnymi-dengami.html)
- **Stablecoin pilot:** Kazakhstan piloted USD-pegged stablecoin payments for regulatory fees — [Cointelegraph via TradingView](https://vn.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph%3A0b3da9183094b%3A0-kazakhstan-pilots-usd-pegged-stablecoin-payments-for-regulatory-fees)

### Inferences
- **Practical beginner stack:**
  - A Kaspi or Freedom/Halyk card in KZT, plus a USD account or card at a KZ bank.
  - Payoneer for international platforms.
  - Upwork Direct to Local Bank in USD to a KZ bank, if offered for KZ, for small amounts.
  - Russian platforms (Kwork/FL.ru): withdraw to a card or e-wallet that accepts RUB from Russia. This is the most fragile link; test small amounts first.
- **Crypto:** receiving USDT from a client is possible but needs off-ramping through a licensed platform (e.g. Bybit KZ or other AFSA/new licensees). Keep records for tax; avoid anonymous P2P cash exchangers.
- **Tax:** freelance income should be declared. The new 2026 self-employed / platform regime ([digitalbusiness.kz](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-03-20/kak-platformennaya-zanyatost-menyaet-rinok-uslug-kazahstana-vzglyad-iznutri/)) or IP (individual entrepreneur) registration are the formal options; rates were not verified here.

### Gaps
