## Q1. Courier/delivery and taxi: requirements, commissions, realistic earnings, own car vs rental

### Takeaway
Platform courier work (Yandex Eda, Wolt, Glovo) and taxi (Yandex Go, inDrive) are the fastest legal ways to get first money: onboarding takes days and payouts are daily to weekly. Since 1 Jan 2026 an ordinary individual can work as a "self-employed" person through the app, with no IP (sole proprietor) registration needed, and the platform withholds taxes. Advertised courier incomes run from about 180–210k KZT/week up to "up to" 500k–930k KZT/month. Real net income depends heavily on hours, transport and city. Taxi pays more gross, but the commission (about 16.5–18.5% at Yandex, often lower at inDrive) and car rental (about 10–16k KZT/day) eat a big share.

### Cited Findings
**Legal/tax status (key 2026 change)**
- Under the new Tax Code, taxi drivers and couriers working through aggregators (Yandex, inDrive, Glovo, Wolt, etc.) are no longer treated as IP. One source says Jan–Feb 2026 still ran under IP and the move to self-employed came from March 2026 ([Tengrinews](https://tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/taksistyi-kureryi-perestanut-schitatsya-biznesmenami-581893/); [Finratings](https://finratings.kz/news/7476-taksisty-i-kurery-v-kazakhstane-perestanut-byt-ip-chto-izmenitsia-s-2026-goda/); [Lada.kz](https://www.lada.kz/kazakhstan-news/143927-taksisty-i-kurery-lishatsia-statusa-ip-chto-izmenitsia-s-2026-goda-v-kazakhstane.html)).
- A special tax regime for the self-employed, including platform workers, has applied since 1 Jan 2026. The income cap is 300 MRP per month, which is 1,297,500 KZT (~$2,915). Platforms act as tax agents and send income data to the KGD (State Revenue Committee) automatically. Since 2026 an individual can be a platform contractor without registering an IP ([gov.kz KGD VKO](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/kgd-vko/press/article/details/218754); [Kursiv](https://kz.kursiv.media/?p=6885758); [Lada.kz on income monitoring](https://www.lada.kz/kazakhstan-news/147837-kak-budut-sledit-za-dokhodami-kurerov-i-taksistov-v-2026-godu-novye-pravila-kontrolia.html)).
- Scale: 981.2k people use the self-employed regime and paid 20.9bn KZT in social payments in Jan–Aug 2026 ([bes.media](https://bes.media/news/pochti-million-samozanyatyh-v-kazahstane-zaplatili-20-9-mlrd-tenge/)). More than 207k people got self-employed status through Yandex services from the start of 2026 ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-06-11](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-06-11/bolee-207-tisyach-chelovek-poluchili-status-samozanyatih-cherez-servisi-yandeksa-snachala-2026-goda/)). For inDrive, one source says 100k+ drivers moved over ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-02-04](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-02-04/bolee-100-000-voditeley-indrive-pereshli-v-status-samozanyatih/)) and another says 180k in January ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-04-30](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-04-30/180-tisyach-samozanyatih-s-nachala-goda-rukovoditel-indrive-kz-o-novom-formate-raboti-s-polzovatelyami/)). The two figures conflict. inDrive fiscalizes income through a Halyk Bank integration (same source).
- 369k Kazakhstanis earn through internet platforms ([Kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/economic/146113/cherez-internet-platformy-rabotayut-369-tysyach-kazahstancev.html); [Inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/bolee-369-tisyach-kazahstantsev-zarabativayut-cherez-onlaynservisi)).
- Pending proposal, not law: on 18 Nov 2026 a Kurultai deputy, Olzhas Nuraldinov, proposed replacing the monthly income cap with an annual one and excluding platform commissions from taxable income ([zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/obshestvo/6539135-nalogovyy-rezhim-dlya-samozanyatykh-predlagayut-peresmotret-v-kazakhstane.html); [Lada.kz](https://www.lada.kz/kazakhstan-news/160933-taksisty-i-kurery-v-kazakhstane-mogut-perestat-platit-za-dengi-kotorykh-ne-poluchali.html)).
- Finratings analysts expected aggregators to withhold about 4% social payments from drivers from 2026, on top of commission. This is an expectation that I could not confirm against an official rate table ([Finratings 2026 taxi comparison](https://finratings.kz/news/9899-kazakhstanskoe-taksi-v-2026-godu-gde-voditeliam-vygodnee-yandex-go-indrive-ili-uber/)).

**Courier earnings (job ads, Almaty; employer claims)**
- Yandex Eda, Almaty: 180,000–210,000 KZT/week (~$405–470) for 6–8 hours of activity a day, paid weekly to a bank card (Kaspi Obyavleniya ad, via search summary; [ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/kurer-123208557)).
- Yandex Go Eda: walking and bike couriers "up to 511,000 KZT/month" (~$1,150), car couriers "up to 930,000 KZT/month" (~$2,090), plus peak-hour bonuses and tips ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/kurer-120370254)).
- Yandex Eda pay structure: "on average from 1,000 KZT per order" (~$2.25), with surcharges for distance, traffic, weather and demand ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/kurer-120738892)).
- A recruitment-agent (park) ad for Yandex Eda offers "up to 600,000 KZT/month" (~$1,350) with self-employed status ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/kurer-123248909)).
- Wolt car couriers in Almaty: "up to 800,000 KZT/month net" (~$1,800), daily payouts ([hh.kz avtokurer/podrabotka](https://hh.kz/vacancies/avtokurer/podrabotka)).
- A generic courier ad (pharmacies and shops) offers 3,000–5,000 KZT/hour (~$6.7–11.2), depending on format and number of orders ([hh.ru vacancy](https://hh.ru/vacancy/131248169)).
- Glovo is still operating in Kazakhstan, not exited, through TOO Glovo Kazakhstan in 16 cities. Glovo ordering has been available inside Kaspi.kz since 1 Oct 2025. Wolt operates through TOO Wolt Technologies Kazakhstan. Arbuz and MagnumGO also run their own delivery ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-07-09](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-07-09/komu-kazahstantsi-platyat-milliardi-za-dostavku-edi-kto-vladeet-glovo-wolt-yandeks-edoy-choco-arbuz-i-magnumgo/)).
- In Glovo/Wolt-related search results, Glovo ads were rare in Almaty and most ads were for Yandex Eda and Wolt (search observation).

**Taxi**
- Yandex Go commissions changed from 19 Feb (year not stated in the snippet; context suggests 2025 or 2026). Economy class got a fixed commission, for example Almaty fell from 16.94% to 16.50%. Comfort through Premier was unified at 18.50%, which raises it in some cases. The separate software fee was removed. Experts see little real benefit for drivers ([bes.media](https://bes.media/amp/yandeks-go-budet-schitat-komissiyu-po-novomu-taksisti-nedovolni/)).
- A deputy said Yandex Go's commission reaches 23% of the fare in some cases. Finratings estimates about 16–20% for Yandex Go and about 10–12% for inDrive ([Finratings 2026](https://finratings.kz/news/9899-kazakhstanskoe-taksi-v-2026-godu-gde-voditeliam-vygodnee-yandex-go-indrive-ili-uber/)).
- inDrive called its usual service commission about 10% in 2025 (2025 data) ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2025-07-31](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-07-31/indrive-zapuskaet-tarif-s-fiksirovannoy-stoimostyu-poezdki/)). In Sept 2025 inDrive set 0% commission for EV drivers in Almaty and Astana ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2025-09-15](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-09-15/indrive-otmenil-komissiyu-dlya-voditeley-elektromobiley-v-almati-i-astane/)). In Oct 2026 it cut commission to 1% in its busiest cities after a regulator (AZRK) warning over surging prices during a Yandex Go outage ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-10-13](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-10-13/indrive-otvetil-na-preduprezhdenie-regulyatora-vigodnim-tarifom/)). It was also onboarding "thousands of drivers" on rising demand ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-10-16](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-10-16/na-fone-rosta-sprosa-indrive-podklyuchaet-tisyachi-voditeley-i-usilivaet-meri-bezopasnosti/)). Whether the 1% rate is still in force in late November 2026 is unconfirmed.
- Yandex Go had a service outage in October 2026, after which inDrive demand jumped ([Azattyq Ruhy](https://rus.azattyq-ruhy.kz/finansy/117628-poezdki-podorozhali-pochti-vtroe-indrive-poluchil-preduprezhdenie-v-kazakhstane/amp)).
- Platform aggregate figures: Yandex taxi partners and parks earned 521.8bn KZT in 10 months of 2025, and average driver hourly income rose 8.6% in 2025 versus 2024 (2025 data) ([Inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/5218mlrd-tenge-dohod-partnerov-yandeks-taksi-vkazahstane-s-nachala-2025-goda-93126d)). Yandex Taxi has 8.5M+ users and an average pickup time of about 3 minutes ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-08-06](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-08-06/bolee-8-5-mln-polzovateley-i-podacha-za-3-minuti-rukovoditel-yandeks-taksi-o-matematike-tarifov-i-budushchem-rinka/)).
- Requirement and risk: more than 70,000 right-hand-drive cars lost access to popular taxi services under new rules. A right-hand-drive car cannot be used for app taxi ([Lada.kz](https://www.lada.kz/kazakhstan-news/150219-massovaia-chistka-70-tysiach-kazakhstanskikh-taksistov-stali-bezrabotnymi-iz-za-novykh-pravil.html)).
- Car rental for taxi (OLX ads, Almaty, Jul–Oct 2026): Chevrolet Cobalt 10,000 KZT/day (~$22), Hyundai Accent 14,000 KZT/day (~$31), "no deposit". Geely Emgrand 2025 from 16,000 KZT/day (~$36). EVs from 14,000 KZT/day. "Rent-to-own" (pod vykup) is common ([OLX taxi rental search](https://www.olx.kz/prokat-tovarov/q-аренда-машина-такси-алматы/); [OLX auto rental](https://www.olx.kz/prokat-tovarov/avto/)).
- A Kaspi driver ad claims 700,000–1,000,000 KZT/month. This is an advertising claim ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/voditel-111021529)).
- Yandex says it has been a tax agent for IP drivers since July 2025, calculating and paying their taxes and social contributions. Yandex Qazaqstan withheld and paid 8.3bn KZT in taxes for drivers and couriers (2025) ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2025-11-04](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-11-04/yandex-qazaqstan-uderzhal-i-viplatil-8-3-mlrd-tenge-nalogov-za-voditeley-i-kurerov/)). 105k Yandex Go partners got social protection coverage ([Forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/articles/105-tysyach-partnerov-yandeksgo-poluchili-sotsialnuyu-zashitu/amp)).

### Inferences
- Rental cost alone at 10–16k KZT/day comes to about 300–480k KZT per 30-day month (~$675–1,080). Add a 16.5–18.5% Yandex commission and fuel, and a renter needs high gross revenue to clear even the median wage (348k). Taxi in a rented car is high-risk for a beginner. Taxi in one's own left-hand-drive car, or on inDrive with its lower commission, is much better.
- Walking or bike courier work is the lowest-barrier option: no car, no license, cheap start (thermal bag, phone, maybe a bike). The "up to 511k/month" claim assumes full-time hours. At about 1,000 KZT/order and roughly 2–3 orders/hour, gross is about 2,000–3,000 KZT/hour (~$4.5–7), consistent with the 3,000–5,000 KZT/hour ad for motorized couriers. This is my estimate, not sourced.
- Late November and December in Almaty and Astana means snow and cold. Weather surcharges raise per-order pay, but walking and bike work gets harder. Astana winters especially favour car couriers.
- Time to first payout: daily (Wolt per the ad) to weekly (Yandex Eda per the ad). Onboarding through the app or a park usually takes a few days. I did not verify this against official pages.

### Gaps
- Official partner requirements (age 18+, documents, citizenship or residence rules for foreigners, driving experience for taxi) could not be read: explore.wolt.com, Yandex Pro and Glovo pages were blocked or not returned.
- Exact self-employed tax rates (IPN, OPV, social contributions withheld per payout) are not confirmed. One article mentioned IPN and OPV "at 1% each" plus social contributions, but I could not verify it.
- No independent survey of actual net courier or taxi earnings per hour by city. Earnings for Astana, Shymkent and other cities were not found.
- Yandex Lavka, Arbuz.kz, Magnum (MagnumGO) and Kaspi delivery courier or picker pay rates were not found.

## Q2. Temporary and part-time work (HH.kz, Enbek.kz, OLX/Kaspi listings), including New Year season

### Takeaway
Day-paid unskilled shifts are widely available in Almaty: loader or general labourer about 10–25k KZT/shift (~$22–56), warehouse inventory about 1,850–1,900 KZT/hour (~$4.2), waiters about 15–28k KZT/shift (~$34–63). Many pay daily or the next day to a Kaspi card, so first money can come within 1–3 days. New Year season adds Ded Moroz (Father Frost) and Snegurochka work, where clients pay 10–75k KZT per visit, plus extra waiter, courier and warehouse demand.

### Cited Findings
- Warehouse loader, Almaty (Satpaev St): 12,000 KZT per 12-hour shift (08:00–20:00) (~$27), paid 3 times a week, men aged 18–40 ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/gruzchik-122812188)).
- Inventory work, Almaty: 1,900 KZT/hour in a cold warehouse and 1,850 KZT/hour in a dry warehouse (~$4.2–4.3), paid the next day to a Kaspi card for hours worked ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/raznorabochiy-122246594)).
- hh.kz loader rates: 13,607–27,214 KZT per job, net. Typical loader rates across Kazakhstan (an Astana listing) are 10,000–25,000 KZT/shift net (~$22–56) ([hh.kz podrabotka listings](https://hh.kz/vacancies/pressovshik/podrabotka)).
- Flyer distribution to apartments, Almaty (ad from Aug 2026): 4 hours from 4,000 KZT (~$9), 6 hours from 8,500 KZT (~$19), 8 hours from 12,200 KZT (~$27) ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/raznorabochiy-120849003)).
- One fair (yarmarka) one-off job advertised 70,000 KZT/shift. The conditions were unclear, so treat it with suspicion ([Kaspi ad](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/a/raznorabochiy-122776013)).
- Waiters, Almaty: 15,000–28,000 KZT/shift gross with daily payouts. Some ads offer 400–800k KZT/month net. Astana: a Japanese restaurant offers from 400k KZT/month net with 1–3 years' experience ([hh.kz search results](https://hh.kz/vacancies/rabochiy_sklada/smenniy_grafik)).
- Ozon order pickers (no experience needed): Almaty about 300–600k KZT/month net (~$675–1,350). Astana from 379,500 KZT/month gross ([hh.kz sborschik na sklad](https://hh.kz/vacancies/sborschik-na-sklad)).
- Wildberries warehouse picker, Aktobe: 7,000–20,000 KZT/shift net (~$16–45). Ozon/WB pickup-point (PVZ) operator, Almaty: 135–150k KZT/month gross (~$300–340) (same hh.kz results).
- New Year: per OLX Kazakhstan analytics, client prices for Ded Moroz and Snegurochka visits in Almaty were 10,000–65,000 KZT in 2024. In 2025 the minimum was about the same and the maximum rose to about 70–75k KZT (~$22–169), driven by extended programmes with contests and photo sessions. This is the price to the client, not performer income, and it is 2025 data ([Bizmedia.kz, 2025-12-25](https://bizmedia.kz/2025-12-25-novogodnij-biznes-kak-vyrosli-dohody-dedov-morozov-i-snegurochek-za-god/)).
- Ded Moroz and Snegurochka services traditionally lead New Year side-gig demand. According to HeadHunter, waiters, drivers and couriers can earn well during the holidays ([Bizmedia.kz](https://bizmedia.kz/2025-12-25-novogodnij-biznes-kak-vyrosli-dohody-dedov-morozov-i-snegurochek-za-god/); [NUR.KZ](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/economy/2052036-kak-mozhno-podzarabotat-na-novogodnih-prazdnikah-v-kazahstane/); [NUR.KZ gifts](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/personal/2052646-gde-zarabotat-na-novogodnie-podarki-v-kazahstane/)).
- hh.kz has listing pages for "animator (Ded Moroz)", including daily-pay filters ([hh.kz animator-ded-moroz](https://hh.kz/vacancies/animator-ded-moroz); [za sutki](https://hh.kz/vacancies/animator-ded-moroz/za_sutki)).
- Benchmark from the Labour Ministry: in July 2026 the minister named a monthly salary below which people should not accept a job (the figure was not captured in the snippet) ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-07-02](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-07-02/glava-mintruda-nazval-zarplatu-na-2026-god-nizhe-kotoroy-ne-stoit-ustraivatsya-na-rabotu/)).

### Inferences
- A beginner doing daily-paid loader or warehouse shifts at 10–15k KZT/shift, 20–22 shifts a month, earns roughly 220–330k KZT/month (~$500–740). That is about 2.6–3.9× the minimum wage but below the national median of 348k.
- December 2026 should see peak demand for marketplace warehouses (Ozon, Wildberries, Kaspi), couriers, waiters at corporate parties, and Ded Moroz work. Applying in late November is well timed.
- Hourly warehouse pay (~1,900 KZT/h) is lower than what courier ads claim (3–5k KZT/h). But it needs no equipment and carries no commission.

### Gaps
- No Enbek.kz data was retrieved: state job exchange pay rates and youth practice programmes are uncovered.
- Promoter (in-store tasting or consultant) hourly rates were not found. Only flyer distribution rates were found.
- Ded Moroz performer pay per hour or visit (as opposed to client price), and 2026 New Year rates, were not found.
- Most hh.kz and Kaspi ad snippets lacked posting dates, so their 2026 currency is assumed, not verified.

## Q3. Small resale/flipping: OLX.kz, Kaspi Obyavleniya, buying from China (1688, Pinduoduo, Taobao, Temu), Kaspi Shop

### Takeaway
Selling your own used items on OLX or Kaspi Obyavleniya is the quickest zero-capital start: demand for used clothing (women's, children's, men's) is high. Flipping goods from China is possible, but in 2026 it carries more regulation. VAT is 16% from 1 Jan 2026. Kaspi Shop takes about 7–15.5% commission and wants customs declarations (GTD) or EAEU certificates on product cards. New EAEU rules for foreign marketplaces were postponed. Kaspi launched a wholesale China-sourcing service in Kaspi Pay for registered entrepreneurs. No reliable margin data was found.

### Cited Findings
- OLX.kz data shows high demand for used goods, especially women's, children's and men's clothing, which makes selling unneeded items a viable way to earn. This is platform-sourced, so possibly self-interested ([NUR.KZ](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/personal/2190951-kak-zarabotat-dengi-s-prodazhi-nenuzhnyh-veshchey-v-kazahstane-rasskazala-ekspert/)).
- OLX.kz has about 9 million monthly visitors, as claimed by the platform ([We Project](https://weproject.media/articles/detail/kak-predprinimateli-iz-kazakhstana-zarabatyvayut-i-nakhodyat-klientov-s-pomoshchyu-olx/)). Beeline (VEON) agreed to buy OLX.kz in October 2025 ([Finratings tag](https://finratings.kz/news/tag/olx-kz/)).
- An anecdote showing how far casual resale goes: after 8 March 2026, women resold gifted bouquets on OLX ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-03-10](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-03-10/kazahstanki-nachali-prodavat-podarennie-na-8-marta-buketi-na-olx/)).
- Kaspi Shop commission depends on product category and is fixed. It is charged only on successful sales and refunded if the order is cancelled. From 5 Jan 2026 the VAT treatment changed but the ex-VAT rate did not (page updated 28.01.2026) ([Kaspi Guide](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/conditions/q4468); [Kaspi Guide conditions](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/conditions)).
- An unofficial blog says the 2026 Kaspi commission table has three rates: 12.5%, 7.3% and 15.5% including VAT. This is unverified against the official table ([konabayev.com](https://konabayev.com/ru/blog/kaspi-commission-2026/)). Kaspi Red+ partners who joined after 4 May 2026 pay commission that depends on purchase amount ([Kaspi Guide Red+](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/red/conditions/q4923)).
- Kaspi launched wholesale purchasing from China in the Kaspi Pay app for entrepreneurs in all regions. Payment is in tenge, and Kaspi handles customs and door delivery, with a quality check per NUR.KZ. Rollout is gradual ([Forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/articles/kaspikz-uproshaet-optovye-zakupki-tovarov-iz-kitaya-0f99fb); [NUR.KZ](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/banks/2354739-kaspi-kz-uproshchaet-optovye-zakupki-tovarov-iz-kitaya-oplata-v-tenge-proverka-kachestva-i-dostavka-do-dveri/); [Kaspi Guide wholesale](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/wholesale-purchases/conditions/q4683)).
- To sell on Kaspi or Wildberries, goods from China need a GTD (customs declaration), an EAEU certificate and invoices. Per mybuh.kz, documents must be dated within 90 days, and product cards without a GTD or certificate may be pushed to the bottom of search ([mybuh.kz](https://mybuh.kz/useful/polnyy-paket-dokumentov-dlya-prodazhi-tovarov-iz-kitaya-na-kaspi-i-wildberries-v-2026.html)).
- New EAEU rules for goods bought on foreign marketplaces (Temu, AliExpress, iHerb) were planned for 1 July 2026 but postponed, with no new date set per the KGD. Personal orders under €200 are expected to be processed as before ([gov.kz KGD](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/kgd/press/article/details/236610); [Caravan](https://www.caravan.kz/?p=19187949); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/novye-pravila-zakaza-s-aliexpress-i-drugih-marketplejsov-chto-izmenitsya-dlya-kazahstanckv)). Over the limit, duty is the greater of the two calculated amounts, plus 16% import VAT (mybuh.kz example). The base VAT rate has been 16% since 1 Jan 2026 ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-05-29](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-05-29/tovari-s-temu-i-pinduoduo-podorozhayut-v-kazahstane-no-vezti-ih-stanet-proshche-chto-menyaetsya-s-leta-2026-goda/)).
- Pinduoduo made delivery to Kazakhstan "free, but with surcharges" (Oct 2026) and is registered in Kazakhstan for VAT ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-10-27](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-10-27/pinduoduo-sdelal-dostavku-v-kazahstan-besplatnoy-no-s-nadbavkami/)). A guide covers buying directly on Temu ([digitalbusiness.kz, 2026-07-18](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-07-18/zakupki-v-kitae-bez-pereplat-kak-kazahstantsam-samostoyatelno-pokupat-na-temu/)).
- Import limits overview: [finance.kz](https://finance.kz/articles/limity-na-vvoz-tovarov-v-kazakhstan); a separate piece covers an increased limit for internet orders from the EAEU ([mybuh.kz](https://mybuh.kz/news/pokupki-po-internetu-uvelichena-summa-na-zakaz-tovarov-iz-eaes)).

### Inferences
- The fastest path, with first money in days and zero capital, is selling your own items on OLX or Kaspi Obyavleniya. The next step is buying underpriced used items (clothes, kids' gear, electronics) locally and relisting them.
- China-to-Kaspi-Shop resale needs IP or self-employed registration, documentation (GTD or certificate) and working capital, typically tens to hundreds of thousands of KZT (my estimate). Delivery takes about 2–4 weeks (my estimate). Kaspi commission of about 7–15.5% plus 16% VAT compresses margins. This is not a "days" option, and it carries inventory risk.
- Buying small lots on Pinduoduo, Temu or 1688 for personal-limit resale is grey if done at scale. Personal import thresholds are meant for personal use.

### Gaps
- No sourced margin data for OLX flipping or China-to-Kaspi resale.
- No data on OLX fraud and scams (fake delivery links, prepayment fraud) in 2026. This is a known risk, but I found no source.
- Whether Kaspi Obyavleniya listing is free or paid was not found. The exact current personal duty-free threshold in KZT or EUR for Kazakhstan was not confirmed (the €200 figure is from Caravan, in the context of the new rules).

## Q4. Services: cleaning, repairs/handyman, tutoring, nail/beauty, via Naimi.kz and OLX/Kaspi services

### Takeaway
Home cleaning is the clearest no-skill service. Private cleaners in Almaty advertise 10–20k KZT per apartment for regular cleaning (~$22–45) and 25–55k KZT after renovation (~$56–124). Naimi.kz is a free app for ordering household services across Kazakhstan, including cleaning, handyman, gardener and seamstress jobs, and is now integrated into Freedom SuperApp. No 2026 price data was found for tutoring or manicure.

## Q5. Benchmarks: minimum wage, average and median salary, subsistence minimum (2026)

### Takeaway
The 2026 minimum wage (MZP) is 85,000 KZT (~$191), unchanged since 2024. The average monthly salary in Q2 2026 was 486,388 KZT (~$1,093) and the median was 348,415 KZT (~$783). The subsistence minimum is 50,851 KZT and MRP is 4,325 KZT. A minimum-wage increase is under discussion, possibly from 2027.

### Cited Findings
- MZP in 2026 is 85,000 KZT, unchanged since 2024, fixed in the Republican Budget Law for 2026–2028 ([Inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/minimalnuyu-zarplatu-povisyat-v-kazahstane-s-yanvarya-2026-goda-a8b2fc); [NUR.KZ](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/economy/2280813-kak-izmenyatsya-mrp-mzp-i-pensiya-v-2026-godu/); [finance.kz](https://finance.kz/articles/mzp-v-kazahstane-v-2026-godu-razmer-raschet-na-ruki-i-chto-vazhno-znat); [mybuh.kz](https://mybuh.kz/news/mrp-mzp-na-2026-god-v-kazakhstane/)).
- Average monthly nominal salary was 486,388 KZT in Q2 2026, with a median of 348,415 KZT ([Bureau of National Statistics, stat.gov.kz](https://stat.gov.kz/ru/industries/labor-and-income/stat-wags/publications/352971/)). The Q1 2026 average was 461,486 KZT ([Zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/obshestvo/6512397-v-mintruda-podelilis-khoroshey-novostyu-kasatelno-zarplat-v-kazakhstane.html)).
- Subsistence minimum is 50,851 KZT and MRP is 4,325 KZT in 2026 (3,932 in 2025) ([NUR.KZ](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/economy/2318204-novye-mrp-i-prozhitochnyy-minimum-utverdili-v-kazahstane/)).
- Labour Minister Askarbek Ertaev said in March 2026 that a formula-based minimum wage methodology will come first and its application will be discussed later, possibly from 2027. A twofold increase "in stages" has been discussed ([Tengrinews](https://tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/povyishenie-poetapnyim-ministr-dvukratnom-uvelichenii-mzp-602560/); [Tengrinews](https://tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/povyisit-mzp-hotyat-v-kazahstane-kogda-jdat-resheniya-601287/); [bes.media](https://bes.media/amp/pravitelstvo-peresmatrivaet-podhod-k-minimalnoy-zarplate-v-kazahstane/)).
- History of minimum and average wages over 5 years: [news.mail.ru](https://news.mail.ru/society/72043734/); [NUR.KZ since 2016](https://www.nur.kz/nurfin/economy/2334258-kak-izmenilis-zarplaty-s-2016-goda-v-kazahstane/).

### Inferences
- Benchmarks for gig income: full-time courier work at the advertised 400–500k KZT/month would be at or above the national average. Day-labour at 10–15k/shift lands around the median or below it. All of these are well above the 85k minimum wage, which is a weak benchmark because it has been frozen since 2024.
- Almaty and Astana wages are above the national average, so national figures understate local living costs (inference; city-level data not retrieved).

### Gaps
- City-level average salaries (Almaty, Astana) for 2026 and Q3 2026 national figures were not retrieved.
- The Labour Ministry's "don't work for less than X" figure (July 2026) was not captured.
