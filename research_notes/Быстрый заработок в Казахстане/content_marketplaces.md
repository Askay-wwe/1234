# Earning money in Kazakhstan (2026) through content/social media and online marketplaces: notes for a beginner

Research date: 26 Nov 2026. USD amounts use an assumed rate of about 520 KZT = 1 USD. This is approximate and should be checked against the current National Bank rate.

Method caveat: WebFetch could not resolve most Kazakh hosts in this session (guide.kaspi.kz, quantaapp.kz, reachy.media). The findings below therefore come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Most sources are seller-service blogs and agencies with commercial interests (1club.kz, buhonin.com, ORA, cargo companies). Official sources reached: the Kaspi Guide via search extract, the Halyk Market partner GitBook and B2B site, seller.ozon.kz, and hh.kz/Kaspi listings.

## 1. Content monetization: TikTok, YouTube and Instagram in Kazakhstan, and how creators actually earn

### Takeaway
YouTube is the only big platform with native ad-revenue sharing open to Kazakhstan residents. As of Sept 2026 Kazakhstan is the only Central Asian country in the YouTube Partner Program (YPP). TikTok Creator Rewards and Instagram bonuses, subscriptions and gifts are not available to KZ residents. Kazakh creators earn mainly from paid integrations with local brands, from barter, and increasingly from UGC (content made for brands to post rather than for the creator's own audience). Content income is slow: expect months of unpaid posting before the first brand deal, and YPP needs 1,000 subscribers plus watch-hours or Shorts views.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok**
- Creator Rewards (pays per view) is limited to a short country list, roughly the US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France and Brazil (some lists add Mexico). Kazakhstan and the rest of the CIS are not included. — [Reachy.media (2026)](https://reachy.media/ru/blog/monetizatsiya-tiktok-v-kazahstane); [lilachbullock.com](https://www.lilachbullock.com/monetizatsiya-tiktok-v-kazakhstane/)
- Typical Creator Rewards requirements: 10,000 followers, 100,000 valid views in 30 days, age 18+, residence in a supported country, original videos of at least 1 minute. Reported payout is about $0.40–1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. — [postfa.st](https://postfa.st/blog/tiktok-monetization-requirements); [openreels.io](https://openreels.io/blog/tiktok-monetization-2026)
- Using a VPN to fake region reportedly leads to blocked payouts. This claim comes from a marketing blog, not from TikTok. — [Reachy.media](https://reachy.media/ru/blog/monetizatsiya-tiktok-v-kazahstane)
- Kursiv (Nov 2025) reports that KZ creators can legally earn through **Effect Creator Rewards** (making AR effects), though this needs high activity. — [Kursiv KZ, 18.11.2025](https://kz.kursiv.media/kk/2025-11-18/zhdr-tiktok/)
- LIVE gifts are available from age 18. TikTok Shop and brand deals are separate from Creator Rewards. — [postfa.st](https://postfa.st/blog/tiktok-monetization-requirements)
- Third-party "clipping" campaigns pay about $0.30–1.50 per 1,000 views. These are outside platforms, not TikTok itself. — [hellothematic.com](https://hellothematic.com/monetize-on-tiktok/)

**YouTube**
- Spot.uz (23 Sept 2026), citing YouTube, reports that YPP is open in 108 countries and that in Central Asia it is available **only in Kazakhstan**. Other lists give 138 countries; counts conflict. — [Spot.uz, Sept 2026](https://www.spot.uz/en/2026/09/23/youtube-talk); [monetizationkit.com](https://monetizationkit.com/eligibility/youtube-monetization-countries/)
- The ad-revenue tier needs 1,000 subscribers plus a watch-hours or Shorts-views threshold. One source says the lower tier (from 500 subscribers, fan funding) is not listed for Kazakhstan. — [monetizationkit.com](https://monetizationkit.com/eligibility/youtube-monetization-countries/)
- Payouts: creators register AdSense with their IIN/BIN (Kazakh personal or business tax numbers), receive USD by SWIFT and convert to tenge. The Shorts revenue share to creators is 45%. The source is a Kazakh blog and was not checked against official YouTube docs. — [raem.kz (2026)](https://raem.kz/en/how-much-youtube-shorts-pays-kazakhstan-earnings-per-million-views/)
- Facebook content monetization is reportedly not available in Kazakhstan. — [contentmonetizing.com](https://contentmonetizing.com/en/youtube-monetization-countries/)

**Instagram**
- Subscriptions and gifts work in 49 countries; the only ex-USSR members are Georgia and the Baltic states, so Kazakhstan is not listed. Bonuses are by invitation, in Japan, South Korea and the US only. — [CHRONA agency (2026)](https://chrona.agency/blog/monetizaciya-instagram)
- A KZ agency advises creators to check Professional dashboard → Monetization / Bonuses in the app, because availability is per account. — [DigitalPride.kz, summer 2026](https://digitalpride.kz/blog/novosti-instagram-socseti-leto-2026/)
- Brand deals and product sales have no follower minimum on Instagram's side. — [zeely.ai](https://zeely.ai/blog/how-to-monetize-instagram/)

**How Kazakh creators actually earn: integration prices**
- Price grid by audience size (KZ, 2026): from 5,000 ₸ per Stories post for nano-creators up to 8,000,000 ₸ per Reels for mega-bloggers. Micro (10k–100k followers): Stories 25,000–90,000 ₸, **Reels 60,000–250,000 ₸** (about $115–480), post 40,000–150,000 ₸. This is a commercial source. — [raem.kz (2026)](https://raem.kz/skolko-zarabatyvayut-blogery-v-instagram-v-kazahstane-ot-5-000-tenge-do-8-millionov-za-odin-reels/)
- A blogger with 900k followers charged 350,000 ₸ for a Stories series and 600,000 ₸ for a Reels (2025). — [Tengri Life, 27.02.2025](https://tengrinews.kz/mixnews/millionyi-storis-skolko-zarabatyvayut-kazahstanskie-563499/)
- Celebrity level (2023, older data): Saken Kagarov charged 800,000 ₸ per Stories and 1.6M ₸ per Reels. An agency figure puts a "good" blogger's post from 500,000 ₸. — [Tengri Life, 2023](https://tengrinews.kz/profitably/blogeryi-rasskazali-skolko-deneg-berut-za-reklamu-504967/)
- Kursiv 2019 (old): Instagram ad minimum 70k ₸, average 300k ₸, maximum 1.5M ₸; YouTubers averaged about 100k ₸. — [Kursiv, 2019](https://kz.kursiv.media/2019-06-14/skolko-zarabatyvayut-blogery-i-vaynery-v-kazakhstane-i-mire/)
- Small creators earn modest amounts: from 500 ₸ for a simple post up to about 50,000 ₸ for a TikTok video from an author with about 10k followers (blogger Renat Israilov). — [AIR Press](https://airpres.kz/social/ekonomika-vnimaniya-kak-monetizirovat-svoj-blog-v-kazahstane-esli-ty-poka-ne-millionnik/)
- Package of a Stories, a Reels and a post with established bloggers: from 1,500,000 ₸. — [Magnetto.pro](https://magnetto.pro/media/article/reklama-u-blogerov-v-kazakhstane-stoimost-i-osobennosti/)
- Streaming is reported as a growing income source ("millions on streams"). — [bes.media](https://bes.media/news/milliony-na-strimah-kak-blogery-zarabatyvayut-v-kazahstane/)

### Inferences
- Realistic timeline for a beginner: 3–6+ months of regular posting to reach about 5–10k engaged followers. Small local barter or paid deals (5–50k ₸) come first. YPP ad revenue in the first half-year is unlikely. Ad revenue on a mostly Kazakh or Russian-speaking audience is low-CPM, so a figure of a few thousand tenge per month at small scale is plausible. That figure is not sourced.
- For a beginner, the content route is a slow, long-term bet. Selling content as a service (Section 2) or UGC monetizes much faster, because no audience is needed.
- A "Kaspi affiliate" for bloggers: I found no public, official Kaspi affiliate or referral program for creators. Bloggers promote Kaspi-store products through paid integrations or their own Kaspi stores. See Gaps.

### Gaps
- No official TikTok or Meta help page was fetched confirming KZ exclusion; the evidence is from agencies and blogs. Creators should check in-app.
- There is no reliable source for typical YouTube RPM (revenue per 1,000 views) for KZ, Russian- or Kazakh-language channels.
- No official Kaspi.kz affiliate program for bloggers was found.
- TikTok Shop is not confirmed as available in Kazakhstan; nothing found.

## 2. Selling social-media services: SMM manager, mobilographer (phone videographer), Reels editor, UGC creator (Almaty/Astana)

### Takeaway
This is the fastest content-adjacent route to income because it needs no audience of one's own. Beginners charge about 10,000 ₸ (about $19) per Reels/TikTok video "under key" (fully done, from filming to editing) and 150–250k ₸ (about $290–480) per month for full account management. Full-time SMM/mobilographer jobs pay about 200–550k ₸ per month. UGC videos sell for about 2k ₸ each in bulk packages, and 30–100k ₸ individually. First clients typically come from direct outreach to local businesses, KZ Telegram chats, Kaspi Объявления (Kaspi's classifieds board) and job boards. Expect 2–4 months of daily outreach before a steady flow.

### Cited Findings
**Prices and salaries (KZT)**
- hh.kz Almaty vacancies: SMM manager with 1–3 years' experience, 300,000–550,000 ₸ per month net. SMM/content-maker with 3–6 years, 350–550k ₸. Personal SMM-mobilographer for an executive, 300k ₸. SMM mobilographer, 250k ₸. — [almaty.hh.kz](https://almaty.hh.kz/vacancies/smm_specialist)
- Kaspi Работа: managing Instagram and TikTok pays 200,000–250,000 ₸ net. — [jumys.kaspi.kz](https://jumys.kaspi.kz/almaty/rabota/vakansii/k--smm/)
- Kaspi Объявления freelance listings: SMM plus mobilography (Instagram management, Stories, Reels, filming, editing) from 250,000 ₸. Reels/TikTok under key 10,000 ₸. Beginner mobilographers from 10,000 ₸ per video. "SMM + mobilographer" with about 1.5 years' experience, 150,000–200,000 ₸ per month. Listing dates are not always visible. — [obyavleniya.kaspi.kz (SMM, Almaty)](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/almaty/uslugi/informacionnye-tehnologii/prodvizhenie-v-socialnyh-setyah/k--%D1%81%D0%BC%D0%BC/); [obyavleniya.kaspi.kz (mobilography)](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/almaty/uslugi/foto-video-semka/k--%D1%81%D0%BC%D0%BC-%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%84%D0%B8%D1%8F/)
- A bank's SME blog: an SMM specialist with a standard task set in KZ charges from 200k ₸; very low fees can signal inexperience. — [Alatau City Bank](https://alataucitybank.kz/business/osim/articles/kak-biznesu-nanyat-smm-spetsialista)

**UGC**
- ORA (agency): a package of 100 UGC videos from 200,000 ₸, about 2,000 ₸ per video. An individual UGC video bought directly from an author costs more. — [ORA.limited](https://ora.limited/blog/skolko-stoit-ugc-posev-otzyv-kazakhstan)
- Average price per UGC video is 30,000–100,000 ₸. This is a commercial blog and the figures may be RU-centric. — [zapuski.com (2026)](https://zapuski.com/blog/stoimost-ugc-video.html)
- hh.kz UGC-creator vacancies: 200,000–800,000 ₸ per month gross, and 250–300k ₸ net for Kazakh-language content. — [hh.kz UGC](https://hh.kz/vacancies/ugc-kreator)
- A beginner's case: 110,000 ₸ plus barter products in one month for 4 videos. — [AIR Press](https://airpres.kz/social/ekonomika-vnimaniya-kak-monetizirovat-svoj-blog-v-kazahstane-esli-ty-poka-ne-millionnik/)
- UGC platforms recruiting creators exist, for example creata.gg. — [creata.gg](https://creata.gg/ru)

**Finding clients**
- KZ Telegram communities such as "Media Community | SMM | Казахстан" and "Digital-маркетинг | SMM Алматы" frequently pass on clients. In job chats, reply fast with a concrete offer that includes numbers. — summarized in [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/marketing/1712464-kak-smm-specialistu-iskat-klientov-v-2025) and [kedu.ru](https://kedu.ru/press-center/articles/info-men-kak-smm-spetsialistu-nayti-klientov-rukovodstvo-po-poisku-zakazchikov/)
- Local businesses (cafes, salons, workshops) rely on 2GIS/Google Maps cards with reviews, photos and ratings. These cards are a ready prospect list for outreach. — [Alatau City Bank: where to get first clients](https://alataucitybank.kz/business/osim/articles/gde-vzyat-pervykh-klientov); [Kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/tehnology/134193/gorodskiye-servisy-kak-istochnik-dopolnitel-nykh-kliyentov-dlya-biznesa.html)
- First orders do not come immediately and take daily work over 2–4 months. Starter exchanges (Kwork and similar) have checks of up to about 15k (RU rubles). This is a single-author opinion. — [TenChat](https://tenchat.ru/media/1042261-gde-iskat-kliyentov-na-smmvedeniye)
- Kaspi Объявления has a dedicated "Promotion in social networks" services category in every city, where beginners list services. — [obyavleniya.kaspi.kz](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/aktau/uslugi/informacionnye-tehnologii/prodvizhenie-v-socialnyh-setyah/)

### Inferences
- Startup cost is close to zero: a smartphone already owned, plus a free or cheap editor such as CapCut, plus optionally a mic and light for about 10–30k ₸. The mic-and-light figure is my estimate, not sourced.
- Realistic beginner path: 3–5 free or cheap portfolio videos for local businesses → 10–20k ₸ per video → one or two monthly clients at 100–200k ₸ within about 2–4 months.
- Official earnings from services require an ИП (individual entrepreneur) for legal invoicing. Simplified regime at 4% (see Section 3). Individuals receiving many transfers are now monitored (see Section 5).

### Gaps
- No data was found on Threads as a client channel in KZ.
- No survey of median freelance SMM rates for Astana specifically; most listings are Almaty.

## 3. Kaspi Магазин (Kaspi.kz marketplace)

### Takeaway
Kaspi is the default marketplace for a Kazakh seller. Requirements:
- An ИП or ТОО (limited company) registered in Kazakhstan.
- Kaspi Pay, with payments accepted through it on at least 20 days in the last 2 months.

The ИП can be opened free in the Kaspi app. Commission is charged only on completed sales. In 2026 it runs about 6.4–13.5% by category (10.9% for most categories), excluding VAT. VAT is added separately from 5 Jan 2026, and Kaspi Delivery is charged on top. Minimal startup is about 78k ₸ (about $150) by one estimate. A realistic first goods budget is more like several hundred thousand tenge. Earliest first sale is about 3–4 weeks, limited by the 20-day rule.

### Cited Findings
- Sellers must be an ИП or ТОО registered in Kazakhstan. Foreign companies need a local partner. Needed to start: an ИП/ТОО, a director's ЭЦП (digital signature), bank details and an assortment. — [CDEK FF blog](https://ff.cdek.ru/blog/kak-prodavat-na-kaspi-kz); [MoySklad.kz](https://www.moysklad.kz/poleznoe/marketplejsy/kak-nachat-prodavat-na-kaspi/)
- The store can be opened by an ИП/ТОО connected to Kaspi Pay that accepted payments through it on **at least 20 days in the last 2 months**. An older source states a different period. — [quantaapp.kz (2026)](https://quantaapp.kz/blog/kak-otkryt-magazin-na-kaspi); [buhonin.com: "how to get into Kaspi store in 20 days"](https://buhonin.com/kaspi-novosti/kak-popast-v-kaspi-magazin-za-20-dnej)
- An ИП can be registered in the Kaspi.kz app. Data appears in the state database after 1 working day. An ИП registered in the app can become a Kaspi Pay partner immediately. — [quantaapp.kz](https://quantaapp.kz/blog/kak-otkryt-magazin-na-kaspi); [wemake.kz](https://wemake.kz/blog/kak-stat-prodavtsom-na-kaspi)
- Some people make small symbolic sales to satisfy the 20-day rule. This may breach platform rules. — [buhonin.com](https://buhonin.com/kaspi-novosti/kak-popast-v-kaspi-magazin-za-20-dnej)
- **Commission 2026:** 6.4%–13.5% excluding VAT by category; most categories 10.9% (12.5% with VAT). Charged only after the order status is "Выдан" (delivered). Rate depends on category, not payment method. Promotions with installments may differ. — [quantaapp.kz](https://quantaapp.kz/blog/komissiya-kaspi-dlya-prodavcov); [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/komissii-kaspi-magazin-2026/); [salescout.me](https://salescout.me/en/blog/komissiya-kaspi-dlya-prodavcov). Other blogs quote 5–15% or 7–15%, likely older.
- From 5 Jan 2026 VAT is added separately to the commission; the base commission excluding VAT did not change. — [quantaapp.kz](https://quantaapp.kz/blog/komissiya-kaspi-dlya-prodavcov)
- **Kaspi Delivery 2026 tariffs** (from 1 Jan 2026, excluding VAT): orders under 10,000 ₸ are priced by order amount, from 49 ₸; orders of 10,000 ₸ or more are priced by weight. Another source estimates in-city delivery from 699 ₸ per order. — [raystone.kz calculator](https://raystone.kz/calculator); [cube-demper.kz](https://cube-demper.kz/blog/komissii-kaspi)
- **Tax:** the ИП simplified-regime rate in 2026 is **4%**, which the local maslikhat (city council) may adjust by ±2 percentage points (one source says up to ±50%) depending on district. Check the rate via hotline 1414. Kaspi commission, delivery and ads do NOT reduce the simplified-regime tax base. The retail tax as a separate regime was abolished on 1 Jan 2026. The simplified-regime annual income cap is 600,000 МРП (the monthly calculation index, a statutory unit). — [Kaspi Guide (official)](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/tax/report/general/q5143); [salescout.me](https://salescout.me/en/blog/nalogi-ip-na-kaspi-2026); [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/otkryt-ip-v-kazahstane-2026/). The Kaspi PRO calculator uses 3% for an ИП ([kaspipro.kz](https://kaspipro.kz/)), which conflicts with the official 4%.
- **Startup cost estimate:** about 78,000 ₸ minimum. Opening an ИП is free. Store approval needs about 30,000 ₸ of turnover. Goods from 20,000 ₸. Packaging from 5,800 ₸. This is a single author's estimate. — [buhonin.com: opening a Kaspi store from scratch](https://buhonin.com/kaspi-novosti/kak-otkryt-kaspi-magazin-2/)
- **Kaspi ads:** minimum bid 3 ₸ per click, minimum daily budget 1,000 ₸. Kaspi recommends at least 2 weeks of promotion so the algorithm can learn. — [MoySklad.kz](https://www.moysklad.kz/poleznoe/marketplejsy/kak-nachat-prodavat-na-kaspi/)
- "Restricted brands" (since Dec 2022): only the rights holder can sell under a protected brand, which needs a Kazpatent certificate. Forged certificates or documents lead to store blocking. — [buhonin.com](https://buhonin.com/kaspi-novosti/ogranichennye-brendy-kaspi/)
- Case (not a beginner, sourcing from a flea market, sold via TikTok plus direct orders, not via Kaspi): fans, 1.885M ₸ revenue against about 677k ₸ costs in one week. — [buhonin.com](https://buhonin.com/kaspi-novosti/zarabotok-tovarika-kazakhstan-kaspi-tiktok/)
- Case (not a beginner): a plumbing-goods importer started with 40M ₸, sold 9.5M ₸ in the first month and now does 120M ₸ per month after two years of losses and defects. — [Habr / MoySklad](https://habr.com/ru/companies/moysklad/articles/1100754/)
- Customer complaint showing reputational risk: a used laptop sold as new through Kaspi Магазин. — [azattyq-ruhy.kz](https://rus.azattyq-ruhy.kz/news/111847-vy-pokryvaete-moshennikov-kazakhstanka-obvinila-kaspi-magazin-v-prodazhe-b-u-noutbuka-pod-vidom-novogo)

### Inferences
- Unit-economics rule of thumb: commission about 10.9% + 12% VAT on it, plus delivery, plus 4% tax on gross revenue, plus ads, plus returns. Total platform-plus-tax costs come to roughly 17–25% of the sale price before cost of goods. Goods therefore need roughly a 2–3x markup over landed cost to be worthwhile.
- Timeline: day 1 open ИП and Kaspi Pay → about 3–4 weeks to qualify → first sales are possible in the first weeks after listing if the item has demand and the price is competitive. Profitability typically takes months.
- Price "demping" (aggressive undercutting) is endemic. Auto-pricing "demper" tools are an industry (e.g. cube-demper.kz), which signals heavy competition on identical listings, especially in popular categories.
- Popular beginner niches are not well documented in sources. Commonly discussed ones (small household goods, phone accessories, cosmetics, kids' goods, auto accessories) are unverified.

### Gaps
- The official Kaspi commission table and delivery tariff grid could not be fetched (guide.kaspi.kz unreachable). Figures come from third-party blogs.
- No reliable data on popular niches, average time to first sale, or success rates for beginners.
- The МРП value for 2026, and therefore the 600,000 МРП cap in tenge, was not confirmed in sources.

## 4. Wildberries, Ozon, Halyk Market, OLX and Instagram stores

### Takeaway
KZ residents can sell on Wildberries (via an ИП/ТОО and WB Partners, with a nominal 300 ₸ licence fee) and Ozon (via the local entity «ОЗОН Маркетплейс Казахстан», with weekly tenge payouts). Both have warehouses in Kazakhstan, but commissions on WB and Ozon (RU-wide rates) are much higher than Kaspi and rose through 2026. Halyk Market requires an ИП/ТОО plus a Halyk Bank account, with commission of 5–18% (19% for 18–24-month installments). OLX is a classifieds board (10M monthly users), not a full marketplace.

### Cited Findings
**Wildberries**
- KZ entrepreneurs register an ИП/ТОО and create a WB Partners account. The licence payment for KZ sellers is 300 ₸. — [MoySklad.kz (2026)](https://www.moysklad.kz/poleznoe/marketplejsy/kak-nachat-prodavat-na-wildberries-v-kazakhstane/)
- Commission: 0.5–28% depending on category and tariff options (MoySklad). Median about 20% FBO (stored at WB's warehouse) and 22% FBS (stored and shipped from the seller's own warehouse), per the tariff API (1club). Another comparison gives 10–34.5%. CNews reportedly says the base commission rose from 31.9% to 42.7%. There are no KZ-specific rates. — [MoySklad.kz](https://www.moysklad.kz/poleznoe/marketplejsy/kak-nachat-prodavat-na-wildberries-v-kazakhstane/); [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/wildberries-komissii-tarify-2026/); [callplex.ru](https://callplex.ru/stati/wildberries-ili-ozon-sravnenie-2026); [1club.kz: commissions up to 50%](https://1club.kz/blog/komissii-ozon-wildberries-vyrosli/)
- 2026 changes: WB changed tariffs from 1 Apr 2026, Ozon from 6 Apr 2026, and both raised commissions from July 2026. — [1club.kz April](https://1club.kz/blog/izmeneniya-komissiy-ozon-wb-aprel-2026/); [1club.kz July](https://1club.kz/blog/wildberries-ozon-povyshenie-komissii-iyul/)
- Warehouse in KZ: "Астана Карагандинское шоссе", with a separate delivery tariff as of Oct 2026. An older source prefers Almaty. Goods going to Russian warehouses need "Честный знак" (Russia's mandatory product-marking system). — [MoySklad.kz](https://www.moysklad.kz/poleznoe/marketplejsy/kak-nachat-prodavat-na-wildberries-v-kazakhstane/); [weproject.media](https://weproject.media/articles/detail/wildberries-v-kazakhstane-kak-vyyti-na-rynok-sng-s-pomoshchyu-marketpleysa/)
- Payouts are weekly; one source says in rubles with bank conversion to tenge. First payouts for beginners may take 2–3 months. — [insales.kz](https://www.insales.kz/blogs/university/kak-prodavat-na-wildberries-iz-kazakhstana); [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/wildberries-komissii-tarify-2026/)
- Risk: KZ sellers suffered about 1.6bn ₸ in losses from the Elektrostal warehouse incident (2026), and compensation is uncertain. — [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/uscherb-prodavcov-wildberries/); [buhonin.com](https://buhonin.com/wildberries-kz/kompensatsiya-wildberries-selleram/)
- An НТИН (12-digit taxpayer code) assigned at ИП registration via eGov is needed for the payout account. — [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/ntin-registraciya-marketplace/)

**Ozon (Kazakhstan)**
- KZ sellers now work through the local entity «ОЗОН Маркетплейс Казахстан». Payouts are weekly to any KZ bank, with no minimum threshold. — [seller.ozon.kz (official)](https://seller.ozon.kz/media/beginners/novye-usloviya-raboty-na-ozon-v-kazakhstane/?__rr=1)
- Commission 5–13% by category. FBO logistics 212–3,050 ₸ depending on volume and price. ## 5. Dropshipping and China-cargo models: costs and risks

### Takeaway
The dominant "товарка" (goods-reselling) model in KZ is: buy on 1688/Taobao or at local markets, ship by cargo from China at about $0.8–3.5/kg by road (12–18 days) or $8–10/kg by air, then resell on Kaspi, Instagram or TikTok. Pure dropshipping works poorly on Kaspi, which demands fast shipping and penalizes delays and returns. Key risks: defects or non-delivery, demping, forged certificates or counterfeit brands (store block), and tax/bank monitoring of personal transfers.

### Cited Findings
- **Cargo rates:**
  - Kaspi listings: from $0.8/kg, or about $0.9/kg by road for shipments of 50kg+ (12–18 days). — [obyavleniya.kaspi.kz](https://obyavleniya.kaspi.kz/uslugi/transport-i-perevozki/gruzoperevozki/k--%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%BE/)
  - ChinaCargo.kz: from $1.5/kg (minimum 20kg). — [chinacargo.kz](https://chinacargo.kz/kargo-dostavka-iz-kitaya)
  - A logistics blog: road $2.0–3.5/kg including clearance; air $8–10/kg, 4–7 days. — [taoji56.com (2026)](https://www.taoji56.com/blog/4)
  - Adil Logistic 2026: from $1.0/kg (Urumqi, Khorgos, Xi'an) and from $1.2/kg (Guangzhou, Yiwu). — [adillogistic.kz](https://adillogistic.kz/en/148-cargo-from-china-to-kazakhstan-%E2%80%94-how-much-is-1-kg-in-2026.html)
  - Price depends heavily on cargo density. — [taoji56.com](https://www.taoji56.com/blog/4)
- Kaspi formally allows dropshipping but requires dispatch within a day; the seller is responsible for service. — [aww.kz guide](https://aww.kz/guide/dropshipping/)
- Dropshipping on Kaspi rarely works because delays and returns cut seller rating. — [aww.kz journal](https://aww.kz/journal/kak-vybrat-i-zakazat-tovary-iz-kitaya-dlya-uspeshnoj-pereprodazhi-na-kaspi/)
- The seller stays liable to the buyer even when the supplier errs. Risks include delays, defects and out-of-stock at the supplier. — [churchill.kz](https://churchill.kz/articles/business/chto-takoe-dropshipping-i-kak-nachat-biznes-na-chuzhom-tovare/)
- Forged certificates mean store blocking at minimum. Selling restricted brands without rights is prohibited. — [buhonin.com](https://buhonin.com/kaspi-novosti/ogranichennye-brendy-kaspi/)
- From May 2026 the state revenue committee (КГД) monitors transfers to Kaspi/Halyk personal accounts by the "100/3/12" formula: more than 100 different senders per month, for 3 consecutive months, with income above 1.1M ₸ per quarter. This is especially relevant to people selling without an ИП. — [1club.kz](https://1club.kz/blog/blokirovka-schetov-kaspi-khalyk-formula/)
- Kaspi may suspend access to services without warning (context: Kaspi Alaqan). Separately, prosecutors have blocked accounts tied to P2P/crypto/drug schemes. — [finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/9105-kazakhstantsam-rasskazali-kakie-riski-i-obiazannosti-voznikaiut-pri-podkliuchenii-kaspi-alaqan/); [digitalbusiness.kz, Mar 2025](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-03-17/p2p-perevodi-sprovotsirovali-blokirovki-schetov-v-kaspi-v-dele-zameshani-kripta-i-narkotiki/)
- RRP (recommended retail price) violations and demping are an ongoing issue across Kaspi, WB and Ozon in KZ. — [marketparser.kz](https://marketparser.kz/pochemu-narushenie-rrc-vredit-vsem-dazhe-tem-kto-dempinguet-na-rynke-kazakhstana)

### Inferences
- Cargo for a 20–50kg trial batch costs about $20–175 (roughly 10–90k ₸). Together with goods and packaging, a realistic first test batch is about 100–300k ₸ (about $190–580). This is my estimate from the cited per-kg rates.
- "Белая" (white) cargo with documents matters if selling on Kaspi, because certificates may be needed by category. Cheap "grey" cargo carries customs and documentation risk. This is not well sourced; see Gaps.
- Common beginner failure modes: buying a large batch of an unvalidated product; ignoring commission, VAT and tax in pricing; dropshipping with slow delivery; copying a trending product that dozens of sellers are already dumping on.
